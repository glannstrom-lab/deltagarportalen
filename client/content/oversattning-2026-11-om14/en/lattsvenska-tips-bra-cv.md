TITLE: Help with your CV: 10 easy tips for a good CV
SUMMARY: Ten short tips that help you write a good CV, in easy English. Follow them one at a time.

Do you need help with your CV? Here are ten easy tips for writing a good CV. You can follow them one at a time.

## Tip 1: Write your name at the top

Your name should be easy to see.
Write it at the top, in big letters.

## Tip 2: Write how people can reach you

Write:
- phone number
- email
- which town you live in

You do not need to write your whole address.

## Tip 3: Latest job first

Start with your latest job.
Then write the job before that.
This is how people often do it in a CV.

## Tip 4: Write what you did

Do not only write where you worked.
Also write what you did.

**Bad example:**
"ICA Maxi, cashier"

**Good example:**
"ICA Maxi, cashier
- Helped customers at the checkout
- Filled the shelves
- Answered questions"

## Tip 5: Write numbers if you know them

Numbers can show what you did.
Only write numbers that are true.

**Without a number:**
"I helped many customers"

**With a number:**
"I helped about 50 customers every day"

Do you not know the number? Leave it out.

## Tip 6: Keep it short

A CV should not be too long.
One or two pages is often enough.
Write the most important things.

## Tip 7: Simple design

Use a simple design.
Use common fonts.
It should be easy to read.

## Tip 8: Check the spelling

Read your CV many times.
Ask someone else to read it too.
You can use the spell check in Word.

## Tip 9: Change it for each job

Different jobs may need different CVs.
Read the job advert.
Write first what fits the job.

If the advert wants "kundservice" (customer service):
Write about your experience of customer service.

## Tip 10: Save it in the right way

Save as PDF if the advert does not say anything else.
Then the CV looks the same on all computers.
Give the file a clear name, for example:
"CV_Anna_Andersson.pdf"

## What do I do now?

1. Open your CV.
2. Go through the tips one at a time.
3. Fix what is missing.
4. Ask someone to read your CV.
