TITLE: Working in health care and social care: jobs and ways in
SUMMARY: Which jobs exist in health care and social care, what they need, and how to get in, with or without education, or with education from abroad.

If you want to work in health care or social care, there are several ways in. Some jobs train you on the spot. Other jobs need a licence. This guide gives you an overview of which jobs exist, what education they need and how to get in, even if you have no education or have education from another country. You can look up pay and the present job situation in official statistics. The links are further down.

## Health care or social care: what is the difference?

**Health care** (vård) is medical care: hospitals, health centres and psychiatry. These are mostly run by the regions. **Social care** (omsorg) is support in daily life: care for older people, home care (hemtjänst), and support for people with disabilities. The municipalities are responsible for these. There are also employers in the private sector, for example private care companies and staffing companies.

Several jobs exist in both. An undersköterska (assistant nurse) can work in a hospital, in a care home for older people and in home care.

## Common jobs and what they mean

| Job | What you do | How you get the job |
|---|---|---|
| Undersköterska (assistant nurse) | Help with personal care, measure and report, support nurses | Care education at upper secondary school or komvux (adult education), or equal knowledge |
| Sjuksköterska (nurse) | Give treatment and medicine, lead the nursing work | The nursing programme and a licence |
| Specialist nurse | Go deeper in, for example, intensive care, psychiatry or children's care | Nursing degree plus further education |
| Care assistant (vårdbiträde, omsorgsassistent) | Support in daily life, simple care, social support | The rules differ between employers, read the ad |
| Personal assistant | Help a person with a disability to live as they want | The rules differ, see below |
| Occupational therapist, physiotherapist | Help people to manage daily life and train movement | University education and a licence |

Read more about the jobs that are often a first way in: [Working as an undersköterska](/knowledge-base/article/jobba-som-underskoterska), [Working in home care](/knowledge-base/article/jobba-inom-hemtjansten), [Working as a personal assistant](/knowledge-base/article/jobba-som-personlig-assistent) and [Working in LSS](/knowledge-base/article/jobba-inom-lss).

### The title undersköterska is protected

You cannot call yourself an undersköterska without a certificate from [Socialstyrelsen](https://www.socialstyrelsen.se) (the National Board of Health and Welfare). The basic rule is upper secondary education with a focus on care. Work experience can in some cases make up for missing grades, but not without limit. There are also transition rules for people who had a permanent job as an undersköterska when the rule was introduced. They do not apply to people who were on a trial period or had a temporary job. Check what applies to you with Socialstyrelsen.

## Ways into health care and social care

### You have no education

- Apply for jobs where education is not a requirement in the ad and where you are trained on the job. The employer decides what is needed.
- Substitute jobs and hourly jobs can be a way in, but pay attention to the conditions. Read [Hourly employment: what applies?](/knowledge-base/article/timanstalld-vad-galler).
- A work placement can give you experience and references. See [Work placement as a way in](/knowledge-base/article/praktik-som-vag-in).
- Ask your case officer at [Arbetsförmedlingen](https://arbetsformedlingen.se) (the Swedish Public Employment Service) if there is education you can get support for. Read [Labour market training](/knowledge-base/article/arbetsmarknadsutbildning).

### You want to study

- **Komvux** has care education for adults. How you can pay for your studies is in [Studying with financial support](/knowledge-base/article/studier-med-ersattning).
- **Yrkeshögskola** (higher vocational education) has education after upper secondary school. You can find the courses at [Myndigheten för yrkeshögskolan](https://www.myh.se) (the Swedish Agency for Higher Vocational Education).
- **University** for jobs with a licence, such as nurse, occupational therapist and physiotherapist. Search on [antagning.se](https://www.antagning.se).

### You have education from another country

Jobs with a licence are assessed by Socialstyrelsen, which can ask you to complete more education. What you need depends on the job and on the country where you studied. For a certificate as an undersköterska, your education is compared with the Swedish one, and you need to be able to speak Swedish, Danish or Norwegian. With education from a country outside the EU/EEA, you may also need a work placement. Socialstyrelsen says what applies to you. Read [Validating foreign education](/knowledge-base/article/validera-utlandsk-utbildning).

The Swedish language is part of the job. Read [Swedish for work](/knowledge-base/article/yrkessvenska) and [SFI and job search at the same time](/knowledge-base/article/sfi-och-jobbsokande-samtidigt).

## What can be hard in the job?

The jobs can be hard on your body and mind, and the work hours can be in the evenings, at night and at weekends. It is good to know this before, so that you can decide if it suits your energy. Pay for unsocial hours depends on the collective agreement, see [Unsocial hours pay and overtime](/knowledge-base/article/ob-tillagg-och-overtid) and [Collective agreements: what they mean](/knowledge-base/article/kollektivavtal-vad-det-betyder).

## What is the pay, and how is the job situation?

We do not write any amounts here. They change and differ between employers and agreements.

- **Pay:** look up the job in [SCB's Lönesök](https://www.scb.se/lonesok) (SCB is Statistics Sweden; Lönesök means salary search). The figures are averages before tax and do not take experience or responsibility into account.
- **Job situation:** Arbetsförmedlingen's yrkesbarometer (occupational barometer) judges how it looks for each job today and five years ahead, and it is updated twice a year. How to find it is in [Jobs of the future](/knowledge-base/article/framtidens-jobb).

## How can you develop?

You can move up, for example from undersköterska to nurse through further education. You can also move sideways by changing workplace, for example from a hospital to home nursing or psychiatry. You can also go deeper into one patient group, such as dementia or palliative care (care at the end of life). Which education and jobs exist at your own employer decides how this works.

## What do I do now

1. Decide if you want to start working now or study first.
2. Look up the job in Arbetsförmedlingen's yrkesbarometer and SCB's Lönesök.
3. Read an ad for the job and note what is needed.
4. Talk to your case officer about education or a work placement. You can write down your questions before.

## Common questions

### Can I work in health care without education?

There are jobs where you are trained on the spot, but what is needed differs. Some jobs, such as nurse, need a licence, and the title undersköterska needs a certificate from Socialstyrelsen.

### Which care job is fastest to get into?

It depends on the employer and on the situation in your area. Read ads and ask a case officer at Arbetsförmedlingen.

### Does my foreign care education count in Sweden?

Socialstyrelsen decides this for jobs with a licence. What is needed depends on the job and the country. Start by reading their page about your job group.
