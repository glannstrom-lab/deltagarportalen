TITLE: Jobs with a future: how to see which jobs are growing
SUMMARY: How to look up Arbetsförmedlingen's yrkesbarometer, read its five-year view and what to do if your job looks set to get harder.

You cannot see which jobs have a future from a headline about AI. You can see it in the yrkesbarometer (occupational barometer) from [Arbetsförmedlingen](https://arbetsformedlingen.se) (the Swedish Public Employment Service). It judges how the situation is for each job today and five years ahead. This guide shows how to look it up, how to read it and what you can do if your job looks set to get harder. We do not name jobs that grow or shrink here, because the judgements change when they are updated.

## How to find jobs with a future

Arbetsförmedlingen publishes the yrkesbarometer twice a year, in June and December. It has two parts for each job:

- **The present.** It is mostly based on a survey of employers, statistics from SCB (Statistics Sweden) and Arbetsförmedlingen's own statistics.
- **Five years ahead.** It is mostly based on forecasts for each industry from a regional analysis tool, and on SCB's population forecasts.

You can look up a job under [Find jobs](https://arbetsformedlingen.se/for-arbetssokande/vilket-yrke-passar-dig/hitta-yrken) (Hitta yrken) on Arbetsförmedlingen's website. The whole barometer is also a file you can download from the agency's statistics page, with a description of the method.

### How to read it

- **Look at the job you are interested in, not at headlines.** The judgement is about jobs, and it can be different in different parts of the country.
- **Read both the present and five years ahead.** A job can have many open positions today and still get harder in five years, or the other way round.
- **A judgement is only a judgement.** It is based on assumptions. Use it as a help, not as a promise.
- **Check the date.** The barometer is updated, so an article or a post from last year can be old.

## What changes the job market?

A few things are often named when people talk about the jobs of the future. How they affect your job is a question for the barometer, not for this page.

- **Technology and automation.** Tools such as AI and digital work can take over some tasks. Then a job often changes before it disappears: the tasks become different and new skills are asked for.
- **Change in energy and climate.** This affects which industries grow and which shrink.
- **The make-up of the population.** How many people are of working age and how many need care affects the needs.
- **How we work.** Working from home and online shopping have changed where and how work is done. Read [Working from home: a guide](/knowledge-base/article/distansarbete-guide).

"Risk of automation" does not mean that all jobs in the job disappear. It can mean fewer jobs, other tasks or new demands.

## If your job looks set to get harder

This is no reason to panic, but it is a reason to start looking.

1. **Make an inventory of what you can do.** Many skills can go with you to another job. See [Skills inventory](/knowledge-base/article/kompetensinventering-guide).
2. **Look for jobs where you can use them.** [The interest guide](/interest-guide) and [the skills gap analysis](/skills-gap-analysis) help you, and the barometer shows the situation in the jobs you find.
3. **See what is missing.** Maybe a course, a certificate or a validation is enough. Read [Validation of vocational skills](/knowledge-base/article/validering-av-yrkeskunskaper) and [Studying with financial support](/knowledge-base/article/studier-med-ersattning).
4. **Get support.** Your case officer at Arbetsförmedlingen can help you plan. You can also read [Changing career: a complete guide](/knowledge-base/article/karriarbyte-guide).

## What can you do whatever your job is?

- **Keep your skills alive.** Small steps are enough. See [Skills development: what it is and how to develop yours](/knowledge-base/article/kompetensutveckling-guide).
- **Think skills, not titles.** A title can disappear while what you can do is still asked for.
- **Have contacts.** Contacts give you information about what happens in your job and in others. See [Networking for jobs](/knowledge-base/article/natverka-for-jobb).
- **Keep an eye on the job market.** [Job market trends: what is happening now?](/knowledge-base/article/arbetsmarknadstrender-nu) explains how you can follow it.

## What do I do now

1. Look up your present job and a job you are thinking about in Find jobs (Hitta yrken).
2. Write down what the judgement says and which date it is from.
3. Make an inventory of what you can do.
4. Talk to your case officer or a study and career counsellor about the next step.

## Common questions

### Which jobs have a future?

It varies by job, place and time. Arbetsförmedlingen's yrkesbarometer is the best help, because it is updated and is specific to each job.

### Which jobs will disappear because of AI?

Nobody knows for sure. Judgements exist, but they are based on assumptions. Most often the tasks change, and a whole job does not disappear overnight. Read [AI in your job search](/knowledge-base/article/ai-jobbsokning-guide) to see how you can use the tools yourself.

### Should I change jobs?

Only you can decide, and it depends on your situation. Start by looking up your job and talk to your case officer.
