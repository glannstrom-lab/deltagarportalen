TITLE: Skills profile: how to document and prove what you can do
SUMMARY: How to turn your list of skills into a skills profile: proof, certificates, validation and how to use it in your CV and at interviews.

A skills profile is a document where you collect what you can do, how well you can do it and how you can show it. This page starts where the inventory ends. You have a list, and now it should become something you can send, show and back up with proof. If you do not have a list yet, there are two guides to start with: [Skills inventory and skills mapping](/knowledge-base/article/kompetensutvardering) and [What skills do I have?](/knowledge-base/article/vilka-kompetenser-har-jag). If you want the meaning of the word, it is in [What is a skill?](/knowledge-base/article/vad-ar-kompetens).

## Why a skills profile?

A list in your head cannot be sent. If you write it down, you get material for your CV, cover letter and interview in one place. You also see what you have proof for and what is only a feeling.

## Three kinds of skills, three kinds of proof

What counts as proof depends on the kind of skill.

| Kind | Example | How you show it |
|---|---|---|
| Formal | Degree, eligibility, driving licence, forklift licence, trade certificate | Grades, certificates or cards. How to get them: [Employer certificates and grades](/knowledge-base/article/arbetsgivarintyg-och-betyg) |
| Practical | Tools, systems and methods you know | Examples of what you have done, work sample, portfolio, reference |
| Personal | Working together, being organised, taking the first step | A clear example of when you did it |

Practical and personal skills seldom have a paper. Then an example is the strongest thing you have: what was the situation, what did you do, how did it go. This is the same structure as in [the STAR method](/knowledge-base/article/kompetensbaserad-intervju-star-metoden).

## How to build your skills profile

1. **Write each skill as one line.** The skill, for how long and how you have used it.
2. **Put proof on each line.** A certificate, grades, a reference, a work sample or an example. If there is nothing, mark it. Then you know where you need to collect proof.
3. **Prioritise.** Put the skills that fit the job you are applying for first, and the ones that do not fit last or leave them out.
4. **Rewrite for each application.** The same profile can give different CVs depending on the job. Match the words with the ad without writing anything that is not true.

### Template

Copy and fill in:

- Formal qualifications: education, eligibility, driving licence, certificates. Proof: certificate or grades.
- Job skills: skill, level, example. Proof: reference or work sample.
- Tools and systems: name, how you have used them.
- Personal strengths: strength and an example.
- Languages: language and level.

You can set the levels yourself, for example beginner, can do with support, can do alone, can teach others. The important thing is that you can stand behind them.

## Verify your skills

If you have knowledge that you learned at work and that has no paper, there are some ways.

- **Validation.** You can have your vocational skills assessed and certified. Read [Validation of vocational skills](/knowledge-base/article/validering-av-yrkeskunskaper).
- **Foreign education.** If you have education from another country, see [Validating foreign education](/knowledge-base/article/validera-utlandsk-utbildning).
- **References.** Ask a former manager or colleague to confirm what you did. See [References: how to do it](/knowledge-base/article/referenser-guide).
- **Portfolio.** For skills that you can show, for example text, code, images or crafts. See [Build a skills portfolio](/knowledge-base/article/kompetensportfolj).
- **Work sample or work placement.** Sometimes the fastest way is to show it on the spot. See [Work placement as a way in](/knowledge-base/article/praktik-som-vag-in).

If you are not sure what your education or experience is equal to, a study and career counsellor can help you. Your case officer at [Arbetsförmedlingen](https://arbetsformedlingen.se) (the Swedish Public Employment Service) can guide you about validation.

## Use the profile

- **In your CV:** take the skills that are most relevant for the job and give clear examples. See [How to write a CV](/knowledge-base/article/cv-grunder), or build it in [the CV builder](/cv).
- **In your cover letter:** pick two or three skills and connect them to what the employer is looking for. See [Cover letter](/knowledge-base/article/personligt-brev).
- **On LinkedIn:** the skills can be in your profile. See [LinkedIn optimisation](/knowledge-base/article/linkedin-optimering).
- **At the interview:** have an example ready for each skill you have written.
- **Towards the next goal:** compare the profile with a job you want, and see what is missing with [the skills gap analysis](/skills-gap-analysis).

## Common mistakes

- Counting only what has a paper.
- Listing skills without examples.
- Putting everything in every application instead of choosing.
- Writing something you cannot stand behind at an interview.

## What do I do now

Take your list, or start one: choose a job you want to apply for, and write three skills that fit. Put proof or an example on each. That is a good enough skills profile for that application.

## Common questions

### What is a skills profile?

It is a summary of what you can do, how well you can do it and what you have to prove it. It can be a document of its own or a part of your CV.

### How do I verify a skill without a certificate?

With validation, a reference, a work sample, a portfolio or a clear example you can tell about. Which one applies depends on the skill.

### What is a skills platform?

The word is used about digital tools where organisations collect the skills of their employees. As a job seeker, you do not need to choose such a tool in advance. A document or your CV is enough.
