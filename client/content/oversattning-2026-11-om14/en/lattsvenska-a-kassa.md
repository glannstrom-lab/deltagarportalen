TITLE: What is a-kassa? How it works and how to join
SUMMARY: A-kassa means unemployment insurance fund. Read what it is, what has applied since October 2025 and how to become a member.

A-kassa means unemployment insurance fund. It is an association that can pay you money if you become unemployed and meet the conditions. Here we explain what a-kassa is and how to become a member.

## What is a-kassa?

A-kassa is short for arbetslöshetskassa (unemployment insurance fund).

An a-kassa is an association. It can pay you money when you become unemployed.

There are many different a-kassor. Many belong to one industry or one type of job. All a-kassor follow the same rules, says [Sveriges a-kassor](https://sverigesakassor.se) (the Swedish unemployment insurance funds' association).

## Are a-kassa and a union the same thing?

No. A-kassa and a union are two different things.

**The union** (fack) works for better conditions at work. For example pay and rights.

**The a-kassa** is insurance against unemployment. It can pay you money when you do not have a job.

You can be in a union without being in an a-kassa. You can be in an a-kassa without being in a union. Read more in [What is a union?](/knowledge-base/article/lattsvenska-facket).

## What do you need to get money?

New rules have applied since 1 October 2025. The money is now based on your income, not on how many hours you worked. You must have had enough income for some time before you became unemployed. Your a-kassa gets income information from [Skatteverket](https://www.skatteverket.se) (the Swedish Tax Agency).

How long you have been a member and how much you have earned affect how much money you can get.

You must also be unemployed and able to take a job. Your a-kassa decides if you have the right to money. Always ask the a-kassa what applies to you.

Did you get a decision before 1 October 2025? Then older rules may apply to you. Ask your a-kassa.

## Why should I join before I become unemployed?

How long you have been a member can affect how much money you get. So it is good to join as soon as you have a job. Do not wait.

## Do I have to be a member?

Ask the a-kassa before you become unemployed. Membership affects what you can get.

Alfa-kassan is an a-kassa that accepts members from all jobs. It does not belong to any union. You can find it on [Alfa-kassan's website](https://www.alfakassan.se). You can read more in [A-kassa without a union](/knowledge-base/article/a-kassa-utan-facket).

## How do I become a member?

- Find an a-kassa that fits your job. Not sure which one? Alfa-kassan accepts all jobs.
- Go to the a-kassa's website.
- Apply for membership.
- Pay your fee every month.

## How do I apply for money?

You apply to your a-kassa, and you do it every month on the a-kassa's Mina sidor (My pages). There you log in with an e-legitimation (a digital ID, for example BankID). Read about it in [What is an e-legitimation?](/knowledge-base/article/lattsvenska-e-legitimation).

[Arbetsförmedlingen](https://arbetsformedlingen.se) (the Swedish Public Employment Service) also has things you must do when you are unemployed. Read more in [Report to Arbetsförmedlingen every month](/knowledge-base/article/lattsvenska-rapportera-till-arbetsformedlingen).

## Common questions

### Does it cost money to be in an a-kassa?

Yes, you pay a fee every month. Ask your a-kassa how much it costs.

### Can I change a-kassa?

Yes. If you change your job, you may need to change a-kassa. Ask the a-kassa what to do.

## What do I do now?

Find an a-kassa that fits you. Are you already unemployed? Contact the a-kassa anyway and ask what applies to you.
