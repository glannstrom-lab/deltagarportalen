Do you want to understand how a-kassa works before you need it? Good timing, because that is exactly when you can still do something about it. This text explains what separates a-kassa from a trade union, what is checked when you apply, and what happens if you are not a member.

## A-kassa and a trade union are not the same thing

It is easy to mix them up, because in practice they often go together. The trade union represents you against your employer: it negotiates pay, watches over your terms of employment, and gives legal support in a dispute. A-kassa is an insurance – an association you pay a fee to, which can then pay you compensation if you become unemployed and meet the conditions. You can be a member of an a-kassa without being in a trade union, and the other way around. Most a-kassor are linked to a profession or industry, but there are also a-kassor open to everyone, no matter what job you have.

## Why you need to be a member before you become unemployed

This is the most common misunderstanding, and the most expensive one. A-kassa is an insurance, and like most insurance, you cannot take it out after the damage has already happened. You cannot join an a-kassa on the day you are let go and expect compensation as if you had been a member for a long time. You need a continuous period of membership before you become unemployed, and that time also has to be combined with actually having worked during it. If you have not had time to build up that period when you become unemployed, you do not qualify for the compensation based on your previous income, no matter how long you stay a member afterward.

If you are unsure whether you are a member, or since when, you can find that on your a-kassa's membership pages when you log in with BankID.

## Two things are checked: the work condition and the membership condition

When you apply for compensation, a-kassa checks two separate things, and both must be met.

- **The membership condition** – how long you have been a member, without a break, up until you became unemployed.
- **The work condition** – whether you have worked enough, recently enough, to count as established in the job market. This is the condition that decides whether you get compensation based on your income or not, and it is checked whether you are a member or not.

The level and the length of what you can get is then calculated based on your previous income during a period before you became unemployed. Exactly how that calculation is done, and which amounts and time limits apply right now, change from time to time through political decisions. Always check the current rule with your a-kassa instead of relying on something you read a year ago.

## Who decides, and who to turn to

It is your a-kassa that makes the decision about your compensation, not Arbetsförmedlingen. Arbetsförmedlingen confirms that you are registered and actively looking for work, which is information the a-kassa needs, but the actual assessment and payment is handled by the fund you are a member of (or Alfa-kassan, see below). If you disagree with a decision, you can ask your a-kassa to review it again. If that does not help, the Inspektionen för arbetslöshetsförsäkringen (IAF, the Unemployment Insurance Inspectorate) is the supervisory authority.

## If you are not a member: basic compensation still exists

If you are not a member of any a-kassa when you become unemployed, you are not completely without protection. There is a basic compensation handled by Alfa-kassan, open to anyone who meets the work condition, regardless of membership. It is not based on your previous income, but is set at a fixed, lower level. So it is the work condition, not the membership, that decides whether you get anything at all. The membership decides instead whether the compensation reflects your previous salary or not.

## How to check what applies to you

This text describes how the system works, not the current amount, number of days, or percentage, because those change, and an outdated number can do more harm than none at all. For what applies to you, right now:

- Log in to your a-kassa and read the conditions for your situation.
- If you do not know which a-kassa you belong to, or need to choose one: [Alfa-kassan](https://alfakassan.se) accepts everyone who does not belong to a profession-specific fund.
- If you are unsure about a decision, ask your case worker at the a-kassa to explain what was checked, not just what the result was.
- Register with [Arbetsförmedlingen](https://arbetsformedlingen.se) if you have not already, because that is part of the information your a-kassa needs.

## What should you do now

If you still have a job: check that you are a member of an a-kassa today, not on the day you are let go. If you are already unemployed: register with Arbetsförmedlingen on your first day without a job, and contact your a-kassa the same week to find out exactly what is required in your situation.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I have to be in a trade union to get a-kassa?
No. A-kassa and trade union membership are two separate memberships. You can be a member of an a-kassa without belonging to a trade union.

### Can I switch a-kassa if I change my profession?
Yes, you can join a different a-kassa. Ask the new fund how any previous membership time is counted – the rules for that differ depending on your situation.

### What happens if I become unemployed the same day I join?
Then you have not had time to build up the membership period required for income-based compensation. If you meet the work condition, you may still be entitled to basic compensation through Alfa-kassan.
