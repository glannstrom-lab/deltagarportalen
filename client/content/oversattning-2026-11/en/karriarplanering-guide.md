TITLE: Career planning: how to make a career plan step by step
SUMMARY: How to make a career plan: see where you stand, set career goals you can follow up, choose a path and take the next step. With examples and a template.

Career planning means deciding where you want to go in working life and writing down the steps to get there. A career plan does not need to be long: one goal, a few smaller goals and a next step. This guide shows how to make one, from seeing where you stand to following it up and changing it when life changes.

## Why make a career plan?

- You get **direction** when you choose a course, a job or a side project.
- You can say no to things that do not lead where you want to go.
- You see progress, even when the job search is slow.
- You decide more yourself instead of only reacting to what comes up.

A career plan is not a contract. If life changes, you change the plan.

## Step 1: See where you stand

Write down:

- your education and experience,
- your skills, see [Kompetensinventering](/knowledge-base/article/kompetensutvardering) (skills inventory),
- what you are good at and what gives you energy,
- what limits you right now: money, health, place, family, language,
- what network you have.

It can help to do a SWOT: strengths, weaknesses, opportunities and threats.

| Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---|---|
| What do you do well? What do others value in you? | Which skills are missing? What do you avoid? |

| Opportunities | Threats |
|---|---|
| Which contacts can help? Which industries are growing? | What risks are there? What competition do you meet? |

## Step 2: Decide what you want

Ask yourself:

- What work, what daily life and what people do you want around you?
- What is important to you: security, development, time for family, freedom?
- What would you do if there were no obstacles?

Then write a career goal, one sentence about what you want to work with in a few years. Help is in [Hitta ditt drömjobb](/knowledge-base/article/hitta-ditt-drommjobb) (find your dream job), [Drömjobbsanalys](/knowledge-base/article/dromjobbsanalys) (dream job analysis), [Vad ska jag jobba med?](/knowledge-base/article/vad-ska-jag-jobba-med) (what should I work with?) and [Intresseguiden](/interest-guide) (the interest guide). If you do not know, it is a good result of step 2 to have two or three directions to try.

## Step 3: Goals you can follow up

Make the goals **clear, possible to follow up and set in time**. The table shows the difference:

| Vague | Clear |
|---|---|
| "I want to learn Excel." | "I take a basic Excel course and make my own spreadsheet that I can show, finished by the end of the month." |
| "I will network more." | "I ask three people in my target occupation for a short talk in April." |
| "I want to change industry." | "I find three occupations that are similar to my current one and apply for one of them each month." |

Use time frames that fit your situation. Some people need to plan for weeks, others for years.

## Step 4: Choose a path

There are often several paths to the same goal:

- **Go deeper:** become more skilled in what you already do.
- **Go wider:** take more responsibility or more tasks.
- **Change track:** move to a nearby occupation. See [Karriärväxling](/knowledge-base/article/karriarvaxling-guide) (career switch) and [Byta karriär](/knowledge-base/article/karriarbyte-guide) (changing career).
- **Start your own business:** see [Starta eget – är det för dig?](/knowledge-base/article/starta-eget-guide) (starting your own business: is it for you?).

For each path: how attractive is it, how realistic is it in your situation, what is in the way, and what is the first step?

## Read the labor market

A plan that should lead to a job needs to be checked against reality.

- **Arbetsförmedlingens yrkesbarometer** (the occupational barometer of the Swedish Public Employment Service) shows the situation for different occupations and a forecast for demand five years ahead. See [Bristyrken i Sverige](/knowledge-base/article/branscher-brist) (shortage occupations in Sweden).
- **SCB** (Statistics Sweden) has statistics on pay and employment.
- **Job ads** show which skills are asked for right now.
- Talk to someone who does the job. See [Informationsintervju](/knowledge-base/article/informationsintervju-guide) (information interview).

Also read [Framtidens jobb](/knowledge-base/article/framtidens-jobb) (jobs of the future) and [Att läsa arbetsmarknaden](/knowledge-base/article/lasa-arbetsmarknaden) (reading the labor market).

## Step 5: Make an action plan

Break each goal into steps: what to do, in what order, how long it takes, what you need, and what could get in the way. Here is an example of a goal to change to a new occupation:

| Period | Activity |
|---|---|
| First month | Take an introduction course and talk to someone in the occupation |
| After that | Update your CV, build a network |
| Then | Look for an internship or a job, and use what you learn |

Put it in your calendar. Small steps that happen last longer than big ones that do not. The portal's [kalender](/calendar) (calendar) and [Karriär](/career) (career) can help you keep track.

## Step 6: Follow up

Look at the plan from time to time: every week for what you are doing, every month for the smaller goals, and once every three months to see if the goal still fits. Questions to ask:

- What have I done since last time?
- What have I learned about myself?
- What stopped me, and how did I solve it?
- Am I closer to the goal?

Change the plan if you have learned something new, if life has changed or if you have found a better path.

## Common obstacles

**"I do not know what I want."** Try things. Talk to people in different occupations, try an internship or volunteer work.

**"I do not have time or energy."** Start with a small step. It is enough that it can be done on a bad day.

**"It feels like too much."** Look only at the next step.

**"I am too old."** There is no age limit for changing direction, but it can take longer to get a foot in the door. Read [Jobbsökning 50+](/knowledge-base/article/jobbsokning-50-plus) (job searching at 50+).

**"I do not have the education."** Map which skills are needed. [Validering av yrkeskunskaper](/knowledge-base/article/validering-av-yrkeskunskaper) (validation of occupational skills) can show what you already know.

## Common questions

### What is a career plan?

A written plan of where you want to go in working life and which steps lead there.

### What is career development?

Developing in working life: new skills, more responsibility, a different occupation. Career planning is a way to do it on purpose.

### What is a career program?

The word is used for different things, for example development programs at an employer or support for people who look for a job. Ask the employer or your case officer at [Arbetsförmedlingen](https://arbetsformedlingen.se) (the Swedish Public Employment Service) what is meant in your case.

### How do I set career goals?

Start from what you want to do in a few years, and then make clear smaller goals with a time, see step 3 above.

## What do I do now

Write one sentence about what you want to do in a few years. Then write down the next small step and put it in your calendar this week.
