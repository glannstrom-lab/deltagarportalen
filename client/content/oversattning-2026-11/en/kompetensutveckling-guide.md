TITLE: Skills development – what it is and how to do it
SUMMARY: What competence means and how to choose the right way to develop it, with a simple plan that works both without a job and at work.

Competence (kompetens) is what you can do and use in work: knowledge, skills, experience and the way you use them. Skills development (kompetensutveckling) is to build on this on purpose, through education, work placement, new work tasks or learning from others. Here is how to choose what to focus on and how to get it done.

## What does competence mean?

In daily speech the word means "to be able to do something well enough to do it in a job". A grade or a diploma is not enough, and it does not always require education. Competence can be:

- **Hard skills:** technical knowledge, tools and systems, languages, certificates and authorizations.
- **Soft skills:** communication, cooperation, planning and solving problems.
- **The ability to learn:** to take in something new and change how you work.

If you want the word explained in simpler Swedish, there is [What does competence mean?](/knowledge-base/article/lattsvenska-kompetens).

## Start by knowing what you have and what you lack

Before you choose a course you need to know where you stand.

1. **Map:** write down what you can do. How to do it is in [Skills inventory and skills mapping](/knowledge-base/article/kompetensutvardering).
2. **Choose a goal:** which role or job are you aiming for?
3. **Compare:** what do the ads for that job say, and how does it differ from your list? This is a skills analysis. [Skills gap compared with a dream job](/skills-gap-analysis) helps you.
4. **Ask:** someone who does the job today can say what is really needed and what is only in the ad.

## Ways to learn

| Way | Suits when | Keep in mind |
|---|---|---|
| Formal education (komvux (adult education), yrkeshögskola (vocational college), university) | You need a recognized grade or authorization | Takes time and may need funding |
| Arbetsmarknadsutbildning (labour market training) through Arbetsförmedlingen | You want to get into a job where there are vacancies | Arbetsförmedlingen assesses if it suits you |
| Online courses and free material | You want to learn a tool or try an area | You have to drive it yourself, quality varies |
| Work placement, temporary job, volunteer work | You need experience and references | Talk to your case officer first if you have compensation |
| Learning from others: mentor, colleague, network | You want to understand how it works in practice | You have to ask |

Read more about the ways in [Labour market training – how to get a place](/knowledge-base/article/arbetsmarknadsutbildning) and [Studying with compensation](/knowledge-base/article/studier-med-ersattning).

## Make a simple plan

Choose a few skills to focus on. It is easier to show something you have gone deep into than a little of everything. Then write:

- what you will be able to do,
- how you will learn it,
- when you will do it, with time in the calendar,
- how you will use it, for example in a small project of your own, and
- how you will know that it works.

A longer template is in [Plan your skills development strategically](/knowledge-base/article/kompetensutveckling-plan).

## Individual skills development at work

If you have a job, you can bring up development in the utvecklingssamtal (annual development talk). Have a concrete suggestion: which course or task, what the company gets out of it, and how it fits in the work. What applies to paid time and costs depends on the employer and on the kollektivavtal (collective agreement), so ask your manager or your union.

## If you are a manager or responsible for skills development in a group

The same order applies for a group: map what the employees can do, decide what the business needs in the future, and plan for the space in between. For example, for a communications department it can mean first listing which tasks the department should manage in a year, and then seeing who can do what. Start in the mapping, not in the course catalogue. Which education suits cannot be said in advance, it depends on what the mapping shows.

## Show what you can do

- Collect certificates, grades and examples of what you have done.
- Update your CV and [LinkedIn](/linkedin-optimizer) with the new things.
- In the interview, tell how you learned it, not only that you did it.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I develop my skills when I am unemployed?

Start by mapping, choose a goal and bring it up with your case officer at [Arbetsförmedlingen (the Swedish Public Employment Service)](https://arbetsformedlingen.se). They decide which support is relevant for you, for example education or work placement.

### What is the difference between hard and soft skills?

Hard skills can be pointed to and often proven: a tool, a certificate, a language. Soft skills are about how you work with others and with tasks.

### What is kompetensväxling?

To change from one role to another by building on what you can already do, often with some extra education. See [Career change – how to change path](/knowledge-base/article/karriarvaxling-guide).

## What do I do now

Write three things you want to be able to do in a year. Choose one. Find a first small thing you can do this week, for example a free lesson or a talk with someone who already knows it.
