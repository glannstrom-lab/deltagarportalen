TITLE: Sjuklön: who pays, and what is karensavdrag?
SUMMARY: The employer pays sjuklön, then Försäkringskassan takes over. How karensavdrag, doctor's certificates and vab work, and who you report sick to.

Sjuklön (sick pay) is the pay your employer gives when you are ill during an initial period of sick absence. After that, Försäkringskassan (the Swedish Social Insurance Agency) takes over with sjukpenning (sickness benefit). During the sjuklön a karensavdrag (qualifying-day deduction) is made, and for care of a sick child you apply for vab at Försäkringskassan. Here is who pays what, and who you should contact.

## What is sjuklön?

Sjuklön is paid by your employer, not by an authority, when you are ill and cannot work. It is calculated from your pay but does not equal the whole pay, and a kollektivavtal (collective agreement) can give more. The sjuklön applies during an initial period. After that period it is [Försäkringskassan](https://www.forsakringskassan.se) that assesses your right to sjukpenning.

## Step one: report sick to your employer

If you become ill, you tell your employer, according to the routines at your workplace. It is not Försäkringskassan you contact first. They come in later if the sick period gets long. If you have part-time work next to being a job seeker, see [Sick when you are unemployed](/knowledge-base/article/sjuk-nar-du-ar-arbetslos).

## Who pays when you are ill, and in what order?

| Who | What applies |
|---|---|
| Your employer | Pays sjuklön during the initial period, with karensavdrag |
| Försäkringskassan | Takes over with sjukpenning when the period with sjuklön is over |

The employer reports to Försäkringskassan when it is time. Your task is to report sick and give the certificates that are needed.

If you are hourly employed or do not have the right to sjuklön for other reasons, Försäkringskassan calculates the whole benefit, including the karensavdrag. If you have just started a new job and wonder if you have the right to sjuklön already now, ask your employer, who assesses it, and Försäkringskassan if you do not get sjuklön.

## What is karensavdrag?

Karensavdrag is a part of the sjuklön that is deducted at the start of a sick period. It is a share of an average week's sjuklön, and it does not matter what time of day you become ill or what your schedule looks like. It is a deduction given by law, not something your employer decides. A kollektivavtal can regulate the details.

If you become ill again soon after being well, it can in some cases count as the same sick period, and then no new karensavdrag is made. Where the limit goes is decided by the sjuklönelagen (Sick Pay Act) and possibly a kollektivavtal, so ask the employer or Försäkringskassan in the individual case. There is also a high-risk protection (högriskskydd) for people who have been ill repeatedly, and Försäkringskassan explains when it applies.

For a job seeker who reports sick directly to Försäkringskassan, the karensavdrag is made on the sjukpenning.

## When do I need a doctor's certificate?

For the first days it is enough that you report that you are ill yourself. If the sick absence continues, a doctor's certificate (läkarintyg) is needed, both for the employer to assess the sjuklön and later for Försäkringskassan's sjukpenning. On which day the certificate is required, the employer or a kollektivavtal can change, so ask your manager or HR.

## Vab: care of a sick child

Vab (vård av sjukt barn) is benefit when you stay at home to care for a sick child. It is called tillfällig föräldrapenning (temporary parental benefit) and is paid by Försäkringskassan, not by your employer.

- **You apply for vab at Försäkringskassan.**
- **You tell the employer separately** that you are away.

Försäkringskassan can ask for a certificate if the care goes on for some time. Ask Försäkringskassan which children and which situations are covered, because the conditions are decided there.

## Who do I call, and when?

- **Ill yourself, at the start:** your employer.
- **Ill yourself, a longer period:** Försäkringskassan takes over. Make sure the doctor's certificates are handed in.
- **The child is ill:** apply for vab at Försäkringskassan and tell the employer.
- **Not sure about karensavdrag or certificates:** ask the employer, Försäkringskassan or the union.

After a longer sick leave, [Back to work after a long sick leave](/knowledge-base/article/tillbaka-till-jobb-efter-sjukskrivning) can be helpful.

## What do I do now?

Find out what applies for reporting sick at your workplace before you need it: who you call and if there is a system. Save Försäkringskassan's contact details. Ask straight out how karensavdrag and certificates work. Everyone new at a job wonders about it.

## Common questions

### What is sjuklön?
Pay from the employer when you are ill during an initial period. After it, Försäkringskassan takes over with sjukpenning.

### Do I have to have worked for a certain time before I get sjuklön?
It can affect the right, especially in very short jobs. Ask the employer, and Försäkringskassan if you do not get sjuklön.

### What happens if I become ill during my trial employment?
Trial employment (provanställning) is a form of employment. The sjuklön rules are decided by your employment in the same way, so ask the employer if you are not sure.

### How does sjuklön work for hourly employees?
If you do not have the right to sjuklön, Försäkringskassan calculates the benefit, including the karensavdrag.

### Can I do vab for children other than my own?
It depends on your situation. Försäkringskassan decides, so check there.
