TITLE: Arbetsgivarintyg: what it is and how to get one
SUMMARY: The arbetsgivarintyg is the a-kassa's basis. How to ask for it, how it differs from other certificates, and what to do if the employer does not answer.

An arbetsgivarintyg (employer certificate) is the document that the a-kassa (unemployment insurance fund) uses to decide your right to benefit when a job has ended. It is filled in by your former employer and is information about the employment, not an opinion about you. If you need a paper for the bank or a new employer instead, that is a different document.

## What is an arbetsgivarintyg?

The certificate should show facts about the employment. Since the a-kassa got new rules on 1 October 2025, the fund gets your income from Skatteverket (the Swedish Tax Agency), and the certificate has simpler content. Among the things the employer confirms are details about the type of employment and the reason the employment ended. Older cases that were decided before the reform may still need more information, so ask your a-kassa what it needs in your case.

The employer fills in the certificate digitally, for example through [arbetsgivarintyg.nu](https://arbetsgivarintyg.nu). You as the employee do not need to write the certificate yourself, but you need to ask for it. An employer must issue the certificate when the employment ends. This is true whether you quit, were dismissed or worked to the end of a temporary job.

## Arbetsgivarintyg, tjänstgöringsintyg and betyg: what is the difference?

The words are often mixed up, but they are different papers with different purposes.

| The word you hear | What is meant |
|---|---|
| Arbetsgivarintyg | The basis for the [a-kassa](https://www.sverigesakassor.se). Facts about the employment |
| Arbetsintyg | Used for both arbetsgivarintyg and tjänstgöringsintyg: ask what is meant |
| Tjänstgöringsintyg, anställningsintyg | A certificate that you are or have been employed, and what you have done. No opinion |
| Tjänstgöringsbetyg, arbetsbetyg | A certificate plus an opinion about how you did the work |

A certificate (intyg) describes that you were there, a reference letter (betyg) describes how it went. If you need the paper for the a-kassa, say arbetsgivarintyg. If you need it for a job application, say what you want to use it for.

## Arbetsgivarintyg for the bank or for a loan

The bank asks for a certificate about your employment and what you earn. That is an anställningsintyg (employment certificate), not the arbetsgivarintyg that the a-kassa uses. Ask the bank which document it wants and ask your employer for exactly that. Read more in [Tjänstgöringsintyg och anställningsintyg](/knowledge-base/article/tjanstgoringsintyg-och-anstallningsintyg) (service certificate and employment certificate).

## What is a reference?

A reference is not a document. It is a person who can answer questions about you when a new employer calls. There is no right to get a reference. Ask the person in advance, and write down contact details before you leave.

## How to ask for the arbetsgivarintyg

1. **Ask on your last working day**, or in a final talk. Then everything is fresh.
2. **Use the a-kassa's digital service** if it has a function for sending a request to the employer. How it works is different between the funds, so look at your fund's page.
3. **Write to the employer** if you do not get an answer. An email gives you a record to point to later.

## If the employer does not answer

- Follow up in writing and stick to the facts.
- Set a reasonable time for an answer.
- Get help from your union (facket) if you are a member.
- Tell the a-kassa that you are waiting. They can say what they need while you wait. If you are unsure about your decision, you can contact [Inspektionen för arbetslöshetsförsäkringen (IAF)](https://www.iaf.se) (the Unemployment Insurance Inspectorate), which supervises the a-kassor.

If the employer does not pay salary or does not answer at all, it can be connected to bigger problems. Then [Lönen kom inte – vad gör du nu?](/knowledge-base/article/lonen-uteblir-vad-gor-jag) (the salary did not come: what do you do now?) can help. If the company has gone bankrupt, see [Lönegaranti vid konkurs](/knowledge-base/article/lonegaranti-vid-konkurs) (wage guarantee in bankruptcy).

## Ask for it when you leave, not when you need it

The most common mistake is to wait. After six months the contact person may have changed jobs or the company may have a new owner. Ask for the certificate at the same time as you hand in your keys, and save a digital copy.

If you prefer to read in easy Swedish, there is [Vad är ett arbetsgivarintyg?](/knowledge-base/article/lattsvenska-arbetsgivarintyg) (what is an arbetsgivarintyg?).

## What do I do now?

If you recently left a job without asking for anything: do it today. Check with your a-kassa what it needs from the employer. Write down contact details for a possible reference.

## Common questions

### What is an arbetsgivarintyg?
A document from your former employer that the a-kassa uses to decide your right to benefit. It describes the employment, not you as a person.

### Do I need an arbetsgivarintyg to apply for a new job?
No, it is needed for the a-kassa. For a job application you may need a tjänstgöringsintyg or a reference. It is good to have both ready.

### Can I get an arbetsgivarintyg if I quit myself?
Yes, the employer must issue the certificate no matter why the employment ended. But quitting yourself can affect your a-kassa, see [Säga upp sig själv](/knowledge-base/article/saga-upp-sig-och-a-kassa) (quitting yourself).

### What is the difference between arbetsgivarintyg and tjänstgöringsintyg?
The arbetsgivarintyg is the a-kassa's basis. The tjänstgöringsintyg shows that you have been employed and what you have done, and is used for example with a new employer or the bank.

### What do I do if I do not have a tjänstgöringsbetyg from an old job?
Ask the employer, even if some time has passed. Whether you have a right to a betyg depends on the kollektivavtal (collective agreement) and the employment contract, so also ask your union.
