TITLE: Shortage jobs in Sweden: how to read the yrkesbarometer
SUMMARY: Which jobs have a shortage is decided job by job. How to read Arbetsförmedlingen's yrkesbarometer and what shortage, balance and paradox mean.

Which occupations have a shortage is decided occupation by occupation, not industry by industry, and the one who measures it is [Arbetsförmedlingen](https://arbetsformedlingen.se) (the Swedish Public Employment Service) in its yrkesbarometer (occupational barometer). There you can look up an occupation and see if it is judged as brist (shortage), balans (balance), överskott (surplus) or paradox, and how demand is expected to develop five years ahead. This page explains what the words mean and how to use the list without going wrong.

We do not print a ready list of shortage occupations here. It changes twice a year, and an old list is worse than no list. Get the current one from Arbetsförmedlingen.

## What is the yrkesbarometer?

The yrkesbarometer makes assessments of the present situation for the chances of work and the recruitment situation in different occupations, nationally and by county (län). It also has a forecast for the demand for workers five years ahead. It is updated twice a year, in June and in December. You find it on Arbetsförmedlingen's page [Yrkes- och kompetensanalyser](https://arbetsformedlingen.se/statistik/yrkes--och-kompetensanalyser) (occupational and skills analyses).

Arbetsförmedlingen has also published the analysis "Var finns jobben? Och var finns arbetskraften?" (Where are the jobs? And where is the workforce?), which looks at how jobs and workers are spread across the country.

## Four words that mean different things

Arbetsförmedlingen puts a label on each occupation it assesses:

- **Brist (shortage):** employers have recruitment problems, and at the same time there is no available workforce to get. The workforce could otherwise be unemployed people who look in the occupation, people who work part-time or people who have left the occupation.
- **Paradox:** employers have recruitment problems, but there is also workforce. This is often because of a poor match between those who apply and what employers ask for.
- **Balans (balance):** supply and demand are about equal.
- **Överskott (surplus):** no recruitment problems, and unemployment is relatively high compared with the number of new hires.

Note that "brist" is not the same as "many vacant jobs". An occupation can have a shortage of workers because it needs a licence or a long education, and then the shortage does not help a person who does not have the education.

## Check the occupation, not the industry

Two occupations that work in the same industry can get different labels. You can read the difference in the yrkesbarometer, where each occupation has its own assessment. This means that advice such as "health care needs people" or "there are jobs in IT" can send you in the wrong direction.

Before you spend months on an education:

1. Write down the exact job titles you are thinking about.
2. Look up each title in the yrkesbarometer and read the assessment.
3. Look at your own county. The assessment is also given by county.
4. Read the forecast five years ahead, not only the present situation.

## The assessments cannot be compared directly over time

Read the assessment together with Arbetsförmedlingen's description of the method before you compare it with an older one. An occupation that has changed label has not necessarily got better or worse.

## How to use this when you have been away from working life

Shortage occupations are not always the easiest way in. Look especially at the paradox occupations: there employers are looking and at the same time there is workforce, which points to the match being the problem. An internship, a temporary job or a short additional education can then be the bridge. Talk to your case officer (handläggare) about what is relevant for you. Also read [Arbetsmarknadsutbildning – så får du en plats](/knowledge-base/article/arbetsmarknadsutbildning) (labor market training: how to get a place).

## Find occupations that suit you

Start with what interests you and can be combined with how you feel and what you can do, and then check the occupation against the yrkesbarometer. The portal's [intresseguide](/interest-guide) (interest guide) helps you find occupations to check. There is more in [Framtidens jobb](/knowledge-base/article/framtidens-jobb) (jobs of the future) and [Vad ska jag jobba med?](/knowledge-base/article/vad-ska-jag-jobba-med) (what should I work with?).

## Common questions

### Which occupations have a shortage?

It varies with year, county and occupation. The current assessment is in Arbetsförmedlingen's yrkesbarometer, which is updated in June and December.

### Which shortage occupations are there in Sweden right now?

See the yrkesbarometer. We do not print a list, because it becomes out of date.

### What is the difference between brist and paradox?

With brist there is no available workforce. With paradox there is workforce, but it does not match the employers' needs.

### Does brist mean that I will get a job?

No. It is an assessment of the situation in the labor market, not a promise to you. What decides for you is, among other things, qualifications, experience and where you live.

## What do I do now

Choose two or three occupations you can imagine, and look them up in Arbetsförmedlingen's yrkesbarometer. Read the present situation, the county and the forecast. Bring the result to your next talk with your case officer.
