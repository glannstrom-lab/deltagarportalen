TITLE: Types of employment: permanent, fixed-term, hourly
SUMMARY: Tillsvidare, särskild visstid, vikariat, provanställning and hourly work: what is different and what to ask before you sign.

There are mainly two kinds of employment: tillsvidareanställning (permanent employment) and tidsbegränsad anställning (fixed-term employment). Lagen om anställningsskydd (LAS, the Employment Protection Act) says that the main rule is that employment lasts until further notice. Fixed-term jobs are allowed only in certain forms: särskild visstid (special fixed term), vikariat (temporary cover), säsongsarbete (seasonal work) and provanställning (probationary employment).

What decides your rights is the form in your contract, not what the employer calls it. The rules are in LAS, which you can find at [Riksdagen](https://www.riksdagen.se) (the Swedish Parliament), and a kollektivavtal (collective agreement) can change them.

## Overview

| Form | Lasts | Ends |
|---|---|---|
| Tillsvidare (permanent employment) | Until someone gives notice | Notice (uppsägning), with a notice period |
| Särskild visstid (special fixed term) | An agreed time or an agreed piece of work | When the time runs out |
| Vikariat (temporary cover) | As long as the person you replace is away | When the cover ends |
| Säsongsarbete (seasonal work) | During a season | When the season is over |
| Provanställning (probationary employment) | A trial period | Notice is given that it will not continue, or it is ended early. If no notice comes, it becomes a permanent job |
| Timanställning, behovsanställning (hourly employment, on-demand employment) | Depends on the contract | Depends on the contract |

The word "allmän visstid" (general fixed term) is still used in everyday speech but no longer exists in the law. It has been called särskild visstid since the law changed in autumn 2022. If you search for "allmän visstidsanställning semester" or "uppsägningstid allmän visstid", the answers here for särskild visstid apply.

## Permanent employment (tillsvidareanställning)

An employment with no end date. An employer who wants to give you notice needs objective reasons, such as lack of work or reasons that relate to you personally. The notice period depends on the law, the kollektivavtal and your contract. The law gives a minimum time, and the time can be longer with longer time in the job, so look in your own contract. See [Notice period and notice of termination](/knowledge-base/article/uppsagningstid-och-uppsagningsbesked).

If you are given notice because of lack of work, you can have företrädesrätt till återanställning (priority right to be re-hired) if you meet the requirements of the law. See [Priority right to re-hiring](/knowledge-base/article/foretradesratt-till-ateranstallning).

## Special fixed term, temporary cover and seasonal work

**Särskild visstid** does not need any particular reason. But the law sets a limit on how long you can have such jobs with the same employer before they become permanent employment, and fixed-term jobs that follow each other are counted together. The limits are in LAS and they change, so look there or ask the union (facket) instead of counting with numbers you find on a blog.

**Vikariat** is employment to replace someone who is absent. A vikariat also becomes permanent after a time that the law states.

**Säsongsarbete** is fixed-term employment during a season.

The work environment act and the holiday act apply to you as to other employees. How much holiday you get and when you can take it depends on how long you have been employed. Holiday pay when you leave is in the holiday act (semesterlagen).

**Notice period for a fixed-term job:** a fixed-term employment ends when the agreed time is over, without notice. If the contract says something else, that applies. If you have been employed for a longer time, the employer must still tell you in advance that the employment will not continue (15 § LAS). Ask the union if you have not received anything. If you or the employer want to end it early, look in your contract and in the kollektivavtal.

## Probationary employment (provanställning)

A provanställning is fixed-term and, if nothing else is agreed, can be ended before the end of the trial period. Read the full rules in [How probationary employment works](/knowledge-base/article/provanstallning-guide).

## Hourly employment and on-demand employment

Timanställning and behovsanställning are everyday words, not special forms in LAS. What decides is what the contract says. It can be:

- a permanent employment where you work when the employer needs you
- a series of short fixed-term jobs where each shift is its own employment

**Intermittent anställning** usually means recurring work occasions, in one way or another.

It affects the notice period, holiday and security. Ask the employer how your employment is agreed, and ask to get it in writing. See [Employment certificate](/knowledge-base/article/anstallningsbevis). Also read [Hourly employment: easy to read](/knowledge-base/article/lattsvenska-timanstallning).

## Staffing agencies

Employment through a staffing agency has its own rules, and a kollektivavtal can apply. See [Staffing agencies: how it works](/knowledge-base/article/bemanningsforetag-guide).

## Kollektivavtal (collective agreement)

A kollektivavtal is an agreement between the union and the employers' side. It can give better or different terms than the law in areas such as notice period, holiday and probationary employment. Ask the employer if they have a kollektivavtal. See [Kollektivavtal: what it means](/knowledge-base/article/kollektivavtal-vad-det-betyder).

## Questions to ask before you sign

- What type of employment is it, and is it written in the contract?
- If it is fixed-term: why, and when does it end?
- If it is a probationary employment: when does the trial period end?
- Is there a kollektivavtal, and which one?
- What notice period applies?

## What do I do now

Look in your contract for the type of employment and the end date. If it is unclear, ask the employer or the union to explain. More about the contract itself: [Employment contract: how to read it](/knowledge-base/article/las-anstallningsavtal).

## Common questions

### What types of employment are there?
Tillsvidare, särskild visstid, vikariat, säsongsarbete and provanställning. Timanställning and behovsanställning are everyday words for how the work is arranged.

### Are allmän visstid and särskild visstid the same thing?
Särskild visstid is the form that has replaced allmän visstid in the law.

### What is a normal notice period?
It varies. The law, the kollektivavtal and your contract decide. Look in your contract, see [Notice period and notice of termination](/knowledge-base/article/uppsagningstid-och-uppsagningsbesked).

### Does a notice period apply to hourly employment?
It depends on whether the employment is a permanent one or a series of short jobs. See the section about hourly employment above.

### Do I have holiday as a fixed-term employee?
Yes, the holiday act applies to you as to other employees.

### When does a fixed-term job become permanent?
When the time limits that are in LAS have been passed. The limits depend on the form and are in the law. If you think you have passed them, contact the union or a lawyer right away. There are short deadlines after the employment has ended.
