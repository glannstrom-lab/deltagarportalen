TITLE: Starting your own business – support and first steps
SUMMARY: Is starting a business right for you? What Arbetsförmedlingen's support requires, which company forms exist, and what to do first.

Starting your own business (starta eget) means running your own company, often as an enskild firma (sole proprietorship) or an aktiebolag (limited company). If you are unemployed and registered at Arbetsförmedlingen (the Swedish Public Employment Service), there is a support called stöd till start av näringsverksamhet (support for starting a business), in daily speech starta eget-bidrag. But it depends on Arbetsförmedlingen approving your business idea first. This guide helps you decide if it suits you, which ways exist, and what to find out before you start.

## Does it suit you?

Self-employment gives freedom and responsibility in the same package. Think about:

- What makes you want to start your own business?
- Does your idea solve a problem that someone wants to pay for?
- How long can you manage without a fixed income, and how is your family's economy?
- Are you comfortable with uncertainty, with making decisions yourself and with selling?
- What is plan B if it does not work?

If you answer "no" to some of these, it is not a judgment. For some people a job suits better, and that is a completely reasonable choice.

## Starta eget-bidrag: support for starting a business

This is what [Arbetsförmedlingen](https://arbetsformedlingen.se/for-arbetssokande/extra-stod/stod-a-o/starta-eget-stod-till-start-av-naringsverksamhet) writes on its page:

- The support is financial support and support in the preparations for you who want to start a company. How long it is given is stated on the page, and the time can be different if you have a disability.
- You should be registered as a job seeker, have looked for work without result, be unemployed or be at risk of becoming so, have a business idea and not have started the business before applying. There are more conditions, among others about debts at Kronofogden (the Swedish Enforcement Authority) and if you already have a company.
- Even if the basic conditions are met, approval is needed. Arbetsförmedlingen assesses the business idea, the labour market for the business and where the company will start. The support can be refused if the industry is one where employers find it hard to find skills.
- You write a business plan and a budget that Arbetsförmedlingen assesses. You apply for the compensation itself at [Försäkringskassan (the Swedish Social Insurance Agency)](https://www.forsakringskassan.se).
- The support includes advice, follow-up, education when needed (business plan, budget, bookkeeping, tax) and mentor support.

It is sometimes called a grant (bidrag), but Arbetsförmedlingen calls it a support with conditions. Figures such as amounts and exact length are on their page. Start by booking a meeting with your case officer, before you register the company. There is also a page about how it is to [register with Arbetsförmedlingen](/knowledge-base/article/skriva-in-dig-arbetsformedlingen).

## Company forms

| Form | In short |
|---|---|
| Enskild firma (sole proprietorship) | Easiest to start. You and the company are the same person and you are personally responsible for debts. |
| Aktiebolag (limited company) | Its own legal person with limited personal responsibility. Requires share capital and more administration. |
| Handelsbolag (partnership) | Two or more owners who are responsible together. |
| Ekonomisk förening (economic association) | Owned by members, formed by several members. Bookkeeping and economy follow the association's rules. |

Exact requirements, fees and capital limits are at [Bolagsverket (the Swedish Companies Registration Office)](https://bolagsverket.se). Tax and VAT: [Skatteverket (the Swedish Tax Agency)](https://www.skatteverket.se).

## Before you start

1. **Test the idea with real customers**, not only friends. Do a small pilot.
2. **Write a simple business plan:** what you sell, to whom, how you reach them, what it costs, how you make money.
3. **Calculate the economy:** what you need to get in, start-up costs and how long you can manage.
4. **Fix the practical things:** company name, bank account, insurance, bookkeeping, any permits.

## Start new or buy an existing company?

Starting means you build up customers and routines yourself. Buying an existing company can give customers and income from the start, but it requires capital and a careful review of how the business really goes. Start by getting the company's figures checked by someone who knows how.

## Starting a company next to your job

You can test the idea while you have a job. Read your employment contract and ask your employer about side jobs and competition, and ask your a-kassa (unemployment insurance fund) if you get compensation. Also read about the [Gig economy](/knowledge-base/article/gig-ekonomin).

## Freelance and consulting assignments

Working as a freelance consultant means you sell your skills as assignments, often through an enskild firma or a company. You need to keep track of invoices, tax and VAT yourself, and of finding the next assignment. It is possible to spend a bit of time each week on selling even while you have an assignment.

## Things to think about

Financial uncertainty, loneliness and administration are what take time from the core of the work. Build a network and expect to sell.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is there a grant for starting your own business?

Yes, for job seekers there is stöd till start av näringsverksamhet through Arbetsförmedlingen. It requires approval of your business idea. Conditions and length are on Arbetsförmedlingen's page.

### Can I get support if I have already started?

According to Arbetsförmedlingen you should not have started the business before applying. Ask your case officer before you register anything.

### Can I get starta eget-bidrag if I start an aktiebolag?

Company form is not mentioned as a condition on Arbetsförmedlingen's page, but it is Arbetsförmedlingen that approves your business idea and how you will run it. Ask your case officer before you register the company.

## What do I do now

Write down the idea on half a page and who would pay for it. Talk to five possible customers. Then book a meeting with your case officer at Arbetsförmedlingen if you want the support, before you register anything.
