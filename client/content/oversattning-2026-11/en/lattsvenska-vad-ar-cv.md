TITLE: What does CV mean in Swedish? A simple explanation
SUMMARY: A CV is a list of your jobs, studies and skills. A simple explanation of what CV means, what the letters stand for and what to write in it.

A CV is a document that shows what you have worked with, what you have studied and what you can do. Another word in Swedish is meritförteckning. The employer reads your CV when you apply for a job.

## What does CV mean?

CV is short for two Latin words: curriculum vitae. It means about "the course of life".

In Swedish you say "ett CV". It is an "ett-word" (a neuter noun).

## What should be in a CV?

- Your name
- Phone number, email and which city you live in
- Jobs you have had: what you worked as, where, when and what you did
- Schools and courses: what you studied, where and when
- What you can do, for example languages, driving licence and computer programs

## Why do I need a CV?

The employer wants to know who you are before they meet you. They read your CV to decide if they want to contact you.

## How long should a CV be?

Keep it short. How long is right depends on how much you have done.

Read more in [Hur långt ska ett CV vara?](/knowledge-base/article/hur-langt-ska-ett-cv-vara) (how long should a CV be?).

## What should it look like?

- Simple and neat
- Easy to read
- Few colors

Should you have a photo? You decide that yourself. Read [Bild på CV eller inte?](/knowledge-base/article/bild-pa-cv-eller-inte) (photo on your CV or not?).

## Tips

- Write the truth.
- Ask someone to read your CV.
- Save it as a PDF.
- Update it when you get a new job or learn something new.

## Where can I get help?

- The CV builder in Jobin
- Your case officer (handläggare) at [Arbetsförmedlingen](https://arbetsformedlingen.se) (the Swedish Public Employment Service)
- The library

## What do I do now?

Write down your jobs and your studies. Start with the most recent. You do not need perfect Swedish. The most important thing is that the CV is clear and true.

Do you want to read more? Read [Så skriver du ett CV som får resultat](/knowledge-base/article/cv-grunder) (how to write a CV that gets results).

## Common questions

### What does CV stand for?

CV stands for curriculum vitae. It is Latin.

### Is it "ett CV" or "en CV"?

It is "ett CV".
