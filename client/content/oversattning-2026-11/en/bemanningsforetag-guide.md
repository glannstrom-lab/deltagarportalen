TITLE: Staffing agencies – how they work and what to ask
SUMMARY: How a staffing agency works, how it differs from recruitment, what the law says about your terms, and what to ask before you say yes.

A staffing agency (bemanningsföretag) employs you and hires you out to other companies that need staff. This means you are employed by the staffing agency, but you work at a customer. Here you can read how it works, how staffing differs from recruitment, what the law says about your terms and how to get started.

## What is a staffing agency?

A staffing agency has customers who need people for a limited time: as a substitute, during a busy period or for a project. The staffing agency finds people, employs them and sends them to the customer. The customer decides what you do at the workplace and how the work is organized. The staffing agency pays your salary.

This is called being hired out (uthyrd) or hired in (inhyrd). The words mean the same thing, seen from different sides. The company where you work is called the customer company (kundföretaget).

## Staffing or recruitment, what is the difference?

People mix these two up, but they work in different ways.

| | Staffing | Recruitment |
|---|---|---|
| Who employs you? | The staffing agency | The company that is looking for staff |
| Where do you work? | At one or more customers | At the employer that employs you |
| What does the company do? | Hires you out | Helps the employer to find the right person |

A recruitment company introduces candidates to employers. A staffing agency is an employer. It is worth knowing which one you are talking to.

## How it works step by step

1. **You register.** You fill in your details and upload your CV on the company's website.
2. **You meet the staffing agency.** They want to know what you can do and what you are looking for.
3. **They match you with an assignment.** If something fits, they contact you.
4. **You may meet the customer.** Sometimes a short meeting happens first.
5. **You start.** The work is done at the customer, but your employment is with the staffing agency.

You can read a simpler version of this guide at [Staffing agencies in easy Swedish](/knowledge-base/article/lattsvenska-bemanningsforetag).

## Advantages of working through staffing

- **You can get in faster.** The staffing agency may have assignments that are not advertised.
- **You can try different workplaces.** It is a way to find out what you enjoy.
- **You can build experience and a network.** Especially if you have been away from working life for a while.
- **It can lead to a job at the customer.** This is not a promise, but it happens that customers want to keep a person who has done a good job.

## Disadvantages to know about

- **Uncertainty.** There can be periods without assignments. How this affects your pay depends on your agreement.
- **You are new at every place.** It can feel like you do not completely belong.
- **Terms can differ between companies.** Read the agreement carefully.

## What the law says about your terms

In Sweden there is a law about hiring out employees (lagen om uthyrning av arbetstagare). It includes these principles, among others, which you can read more about on the website of [Riksdagen (the Swedish Parliament)](https://www.riksdagen.se):

- You should have the same basic working and employment conditions as if you had been employed directly by the customer company. This applies, for example, to working hours and holidays. The rules about pay have their own exceptions.
- The customer company should give you access to shared areas, for example the staff room, on the same terms as its own employees, unless there are special reasons against it.
- The customer company should tell you about vacant jobs at their company.
- The staffing agency may not stop you from taking a job with the customer.

There are exceptions, and a kollektivavtal (collective agreement) can change how the rules are applied. This is why it is important to ask how your company does it.

## Kollektivavtal: ask before you sign

Ask if the staffing agency has a kollektivavtal (collective agreement). An agreement can give you, among other things, insurance, pension and rules about pay. Some agreements mean pay even between assignments, others do not. You can read more about what an agreement means in [Collective agreement – what it means for you](/knowledge-base/article/kollektivavtal-vad-det-betyder).

You can be employed until further notice or for a fixed time, depending on the agreement. Read about the difference in [Types of employment](/knowledge-base/article/anstallningsformer-guide), and ask in [Employment contract and LAS](/knowledge-base/article/las-anstallningsavtal) if it is unclear. (LAS is the Swedish Employment Protection Act.)

## Questions you can ask before you say yes

- What type of employment am I offered?
- What happens to my pay between assignments?
- How long does an assignment usually last?
- Do you have a kollektivavtal?
- Who do I contact at the staffing agency and who at the customer if something happens?
- How do I report working hours and how is pay paid out?

## How to get more assignments

- **Register with more than one company.** They have different customers.
- **Keep your profile up to date.** Look at [your CV](/cv) from time to time.
- **Answer when they contact you.** Quick answers make it easier for them to choose you.
- **Say what you want and do not want.** It becomes easier to find something that fits.
- **Do your best at the workplace.** The customer gives feedback to the staffing agency.
- **Get in touch.** Ask how it is going and remind them that you exist.

## Warning signs

Be careful if the company:

- Wants you to pay to get assignments
- Wants you to send invoices with your own company, when you should be employed
- Cannot answer clearly how your employment looks
- Does not give clear information about who the employer is

If you feel unsure, you can ask the union or [Arbetsförmedlingen (the Swedish Public Employment Service)](https://arbetsformedlingen.se).

## Frequently asked questions

### Are staffing agencies reliable?
It depends on the company. Check that they answer the questions above clearly, and that you get a written agreement.

### Can I say no to an assignment?
You can, but it may affect what you are offered next time, and it may matter for your agreement. Ask how it works before you sign.

### What happens between assignments?
It is written in your agreement. If you have questions, ask the staffing agency or the union.

### Can I be employed by the customer company?
It can happen. The customer company should tell you about vacant jobs, and the staffing agency may not stop you from accepting one. See also [Probationary employment](/knowledge-base/article/provanstallning-guide) if they offer that.

### Is staffing a good way to get a job?
It can be a way to get in, but it does not suit everyone. Think about how your finances are affected by assignments that can be shorter.

## What do I do now

Choose one or two staffing agencies that seem to have assignments in your field. Read what they write about employment terms, register and ask the questions above. If you have an [application](/applications) in progress, write down where you have registered so that you keep track.
