TITLE: Probationary employment: rules, holiday and ending it
SUMMARY: What applies in a provanställning: pay, holiday, how it ends, if it can be extended and when it becomes permanent employment.

A provanställning (probationary employment) is a fixed-term employment where you and the employer try each other out. You have pay according to the contract and work like other employees. If neither of you has given notice that the employment will not continue when the trial period ends, it becomes a tillsvidareanställning (permanent employment). This is in lagen om anställningsskydd (LAS, the Employment Protection Act).

What is easy to miss is the kollektivavtal (collective agreement). If your workplace has one, it can change the rules for notice and early ending, so read your agreement and ask the union (facket).

## What a probationary employment is

LAS is the law that governs this, and you can find it at [Riksdagen](https://www.riksdagen.se) (the Swedish Parliament). The law allows fixed-term probationary employment, but sets a limit on how long the trial period may be. The limit is in the law and can change, so read it there or ask the union. The trial period should be in your employment contract. A written contract makes it easier for you to know when the trial period ends. How a contract should look is covered in [Employment contract: how to read it](/knowledge-base/article/las-anstallningsavtal) and [Employment certificate](/knowledge-base/article/anstallningsbevis).

## What applies to you as a probationary employee

You are really employed. Among other things, this means that:

- you have pay according to the contract, also during the trial period
- the discrimination act and the work environment act apply, so the employer may not end the employment for discriminatory reasons
- you are covered by the kollektivavtal if the workplace has one
- the sjuklönelagen (sick pay act) applies if its conditions are met. See [Sick pay, qualifying day and VAB](/knowledge-base/article/sjuklon-karens-och-vab)

What is different is how the employment can be ended. See below.

## Holiday during probationary employment

The holiday act (semesterlagen) also applies to you as a probationary employee. You earn holiday during an earning year, and how much you can take out during a short employment depends on when you started and on the contract. If the employment ends before you have taken out what you have earned, you have the right to holiday pay. How large the pay is is in the holiday act or in the kollektivavtal. Ask the employer to show how your holiday is calculated, or ask the union.

## How a probationary employment can end

There are three ways:

1. **The trial period runs out and nobody says anything.** Then, according to LAS, the employment turns into a permanent employment.
2. **Someone does not want to continue.** The employer or you give notice to the other side that the employment will not continue after the trial period. According to LAS, the notice must be given at the latest when the trial period ends. If the employer wants to end the employment, the employer must also first inform you a certain time in advance and at the same time warn the union if you are a union member. You have the right to ask for a discussion (överläggning). The time is in 31 § LAS, and a kollektivavtal can have other rules. Check your agreement.
3. **The employment is ended early.** If nothing else is agreed, a probationary employment may be ended before the end of the trial period. This is not the same as notice (uppsägning), so the requirement for objective reasons in LAS does not apply in the same way. But the discrimination act applies, and the same requirement of notice in advance as above applies if the employer wants to end it. The contract or the kollektivavtal can also have its own rules. Ask the union about your situation.

You can ask for an explanation and for a certificate. See [Employer's certificate](/knowledge-base/article/arbetsgivarintyg-och-betyg).

## Extending a probationary employment

The law says that the trial period may be at most a certain time. So it is not possible to extend a probationary employment over the limit of the law by agreeing on it. If the employer asks about continuing in another way, for example with another fixed-term employment, that is a new contract with its own rules. Then it is extra important to read [Types of employment](/knowledge-base/article/anstallningsformer-guide) and to ask the union. The kollektivavtal can also have its own rules about trial periods.

## Tips to get something out of the time

- Ask at the start what is expected of you, and ask about a follow-up meeting.
- Write down what you have learned and what has gone well.
- Ask in good time how your manager sees the future. Do not ask in the last week.
- You also judge the workplace. Do you like it, and do you see a future there?

## What do I do now

Look in your employment contract for when the trial period ends and which kollektivavtal applies. Put the date in your calendar. Ask for a meeting with your manager in good time before. If you are unsure about something, ask the union or the employer.

## Common questions

### Do you get pay during a probationary employment?
Yes, you have pay according to the contract and the kollektivavtal, like all other employees.

### What applies in a probationary employment?
See above. In short: LAS governs, a kollektivavtal can change it, and the trial period and how the employment can end should be in the contract.

### Can the probationary employment be extended after the trial period?
Not by extending the trial period over the limit of the law. Read the section about extending above.

### How does holiday work in a probationary employment?
The holiday act applies. See the section about holiday above.

### What is the difference between a probationary employment and a normal employment?
A probationary employment is fixed-term and can be ended in a way that a permanent employment cannot. Pay, work environment and protection against discrimination apply in the same way.

### Must the employer give notice that I cannot stay?
Yes. If the employer does not want the employment to continue, the notice must be given at the latest when the trial period ends, and the employer must inform you a certain time in advance according to 31 § LAS. A kollektivavtal can have other rules.
