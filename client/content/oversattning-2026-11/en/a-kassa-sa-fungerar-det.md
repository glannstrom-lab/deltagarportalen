TITLE: How a-kassa works: membership, conditions, application
SUMMARY: What a-kassa is, how to join, when you have a right to benefit since the reform on 1 October 2025, and who decides. Join before you become unemployed.

A-kassa (unemployment insurance fund) is insurance against unemployment. You join an arbetslöshetskassa (unemployment insurance fund), pay a membership fee and can get benefit if you become unemployed and meet the conditions. The a-kassa makes the decision, and since 1 October 2025 the benefit is based on your income. Here you can read how it works, how to join and when you have a right to benefit.

## What is a-kassa?

A-kassa means arbetslöshetskassa. There are several funds, which are connected to an occupation or an industry, and one fund that is open to all occupations: [Alfa-kassan](https://www.alfakassan.se). The list of funds is at [Sveriges a-kassor](https://www.sverigesakassor.se). The supervision of the funds is done by [Inspektionen för arbetslöshetsförsäkringen (IAF)](https://www.iaf.se) (the Unemployment Insurance Inspectorate).

The a-kassa is not the same as the union (facket). The union negotiates and supports you in matters about the job, the a-kassa pays benefit when you are unemployed. You can be a member of one without the other, see [A-kassa utan facket](/knowledge-base/article/a-kassa-utan-facket) (a-kassa without a union). If you prefer to read in easy Swedish, there is [Vad är a-kassa?](/knowledge-base/article/lattsvenska-a-kassa) (what is a-kassa?).

## How do I join the a-kassa?

You apply for membership at the fund that covers your occupation, through a form on the fund's website. Choose the fund that fits the work you have or last had. If none fits, you join Alfa-kassan. The membership fee is set by each fund and is paid to the fund. If you are not sure whether you are already a member, and since when, it is shown on the fund's pages after you log in.

## When do you have a right to a-kassa?

The fund decides the right, and several things are checked separately.

- **The income condition (inkomstvillkoret).** Since the reform on 1 October 2025, an income condition applies instead of the older arbetsvillkor (work condition), which was based on hours worked. The a-kassa gets the income information from Skatteverket (the Swedish Tax Agency), so what counts is what your employer has reported.
- **That you are registered at Arbetsförmedlingen** and are available for the labor market, that is, you can and want to take a job. [Arbetsförmedlingen](https://arbetsformedlingen.se) (the Swedish Public Employment Service) checks that you actively look for work. See [Skriva in dig på Arbetsförmedlingen](/knowledge-base/article/skriva-in-dig-arbetsformedlingen) (registering at Arbetsförmedlingen).
- **Membership time.** It affects which level the benefit gets, not whether you can get anything.

How long and how much benefit is paid follows from your income and your membership time. The amounts and time limits change through political decisions, so always look at your fund's own page instead of something you read a year ago. This also applies if you get a decision: the rules depend on when the decision was made.

## Join the a-kassa before you become unemployed

Think of the a-kassa as insurance that you take out in advance. How long you have been a member when you become unemployed decides the benefit level, and that time cannot be bought afterwards. This is the most common misunderstanding, and the most expensive.

## If I am not a member

You are not completely without options. If you meet the income condition, you can apply for benefit at Alfa-kassan even without membership. The benefit then ends up at the lowest level, and how it is calculated is explained by Alfa-kassan. The income condition decides whether you can get anything, and membership decides the level.

## How do I apply for benefit?

Benefit is applied for every month at your a-kassa, with a månadsansökan (monthly application) where you state which days you have been looking for work. The kassakort (benefit card) no longer exists. Sick days and days when you were not available for the labor market should not be counted, see [Sjuk när du är arbetslös](/knowledge-base/article/sjuk-nar-du-ar-arbetslos) (sick while unemployed). If you need an arbetsgivarintyg from your last job, read [Arbetsgivarintyg – vad är det?](/knowledge-base/article/arbetsgivarintyg-och-betyg) (arbetsgivarintyg: what is it?).

## Who decides about my benefit?

Your a-kassa. Arbetsförmedlingen confirms and checks that you are registered and look for work, but the fund makes the decision and the payment. If you are not happy with a decision, you can ask the fund to review it. Quitting yourself can affect the benefit, which is covered in [Säga upp sig själv – vad händer med a-kassan](/knowledge-base/article/saga-upp-sig-och-a-kassa) (quitting yourself: what happens to the a-kassa).

## What do I do now?

If you have a job: check today that you are a member of an a-kassa. If you are unemployed: register at Arbetsförmedlingen on the first day and contact your fund that week. Always ask the fund about your own conditions, and ask the case officer to explain what was checked, not only what the decision was.

## Common questions

### How does a-kassa work?
You are a member of an arbetslöshetskassa and pay a fee. If you become unemployed, the fund decides your right to benefit based on, among other things, the income condition, and the membership time affects the level.

### What is the difference between a-kassa and a union?
The a-kassa is unemployment insurance. The union negotiates conditions and supports you in matters about the job. You join them separately.

### Can I change a-kassa if I change occupation?
Yes, but ask the new fund how your earlier membership time is counted.

### What happens if I become unemployed on the same day I join?
Then you have no membership time. You can still apply for benefit if you meet the income condition, but the fund decides which level applies.

### Do I have to be in a union to get a-kassa?
No. They are two separate memberships.
