TITLE: Going back to work after sick leave: how it works
SUMMARY: The rehabilitation chain, work training, work trial and step-by-step return. Also what applies if you are on sick leave again after a long absence.

Going back to work after a long sick leave can happen in steps, and it is [Försäkringskassan (the Swedish Social Insurance Agency)](https://www.forsakringskassan.se) that assesses your ability to work during this time. What you can do is find out where you are in the assessment, and ask for a step-by-step return at a pace you can manage. Here is how it works, and what happens if you are put on sick leave again.

## The rehabilitation chain: how your ability to work is assessed

The rehabilitation chain (rehabiliteringskedjan) is not a schedule you can follow in a calendar. It is a principle: the longer a sick leave lasts, the wider the question that is assessed. At the start, the assessment is about whether you can do your usual work tasks or other tasks with your employer. Later the question widens to whether you can do any work on the regular labour market.

The limits between the steps and how long each step lasts change, so we do not give any days here. What is stable is the mechanism. The current order is described by Försäkringskassan, and you have the right to understand where you are. Ask your case officer directly.

This is what the roles look like according to Försäkringskassan:

- The employer makes a plan for return to work.
- Försäkringskassan assesses your ability to work and your right to benefit, and can call a review meeting.
- When needed, Försäkringskassan asks for an investigation and checks if you can manage work on the regular labour market.
- Försäkringskassan can coordinate measures that are needed for you to come back, for example work training.

## Back to the same employer or looking for a new job

| | Back to the same employer | Looking for a new job |
|---|---|---|
| Who knows your history | The employer | No one, you choose what you tell |
| Adjustments | Can be built on what already exists | Must be raised from the start |
| Rehabilitation responsibility | The employer has a responsibility for the plan | It lies with you and Försäkringskassan |
| Risk | That old patterns come back | That no one knows what you need |

Which way is possible depends on your situation and on Försäkringskassan's assessment of your ability to work. Bring it up with your case officer before you decide. Going back can feel safe, but only if what made you ill has really been dealt with. Looking for a new job gives a fresh start, but you have to carry the information about what you need yourself.

## Starting work after sick leave: step-by-step return and work trial

The return can happen in steps, so that working time is built up slowly at the pace your ability to work can manage. Some things to keep track of:

- **Who owns the plan.** Is it you, the employer, Försäkringskassan or the health care that decided the pace?
- **What happens if a step does not go well.** A good plan has a way back.
- **That the follow-up meetings really happen.** Without follow-up the step-by-step return stops or goes too fast.

### Work trial (arbetsprövning) with full sickness benefit

If you are employed and have full sickness benefit (sjukpenning), you can, according to Försäkringskassan, try working to see what you can manage. It is voluntary and is based on an agreement between you and the employer, so you can say no. You keep your full sickness benefit during an approved work trial. But this only applies if the conditions are met and you have reported it in advance. If you try to work without this, your work effort can affect your right to sickness benefit. So always make the report before you start, and read the conditions on Försäkringskassan's page.

## Work training after sick leave

Work training (arbetsträning) is something different from a work trial. There you practice tasks with lower demands, according to a plan that Försäkringskassan makes together with you, the doctor and the employer or Arbetsförmedlingen (the Swedish Public Employment Service). Read [Work training: what it means](/knowledge-base/article/arbetstraning-vad-innebar-det). The assessment of how much you can manage can also be made in a [work capacity assessment](/knowledge-base/article/arbetsformagebedomning-vad-hander).

## On sick leave again after a long sick leave

If you become ill again soon after being on sick leave, the periods can be counted together according to Försäkringskassan's guidelines. There are rules that stop people from ending a sick period only to start again in the chain. How it will be for you depends on how long the break was and what you did during it. Ask Försäkringskassan before you decide to, for example, take a leave of absence or quit.

If you are a job seeker when you become ill, special rules about reporting apply. Read [Sick when you are unemployed](/knowledge-base/article/sjuk-nar-du-ar-arbetslos) and [Easy Swedish: reporting sick](/knowledge-base/article/lattsvenska-sjukanmalan).

## What you tell a new employer

You do not have to tell why you have been on sick leave. For an employer, what is relevant is what you need to manage the job, not the diagnosis. You may need to tell about adjusted working hours or workload, about tasks you want to do in a different way, and about contact with a case officer that the employer needs to coordinate with. How to tell about a gap in your CV is in [Gaps in your CV](/knowledge-base/article/luckor-i-cv).

## How to know that you are going too fast

It is easy to want to prove that you are really back. Some signs that the pace is too high:

- You are exhausted after the work day as a pattern, not only sometimes.
- You take work home in a way you did not earlier in the return.
- Your sleep or mood gets worse at the same time as your working time increases.
- You avoid saying that it is heavy, so that you do not set the process back.

None of this is a failure. It is information that the pace needs to be adjusted.

## Common questions

### How do I start working after sick leave?

Talk to your employer and Försäkringskassan about a plan. The employer must make a plan for return, and Försäkringskassan can coordinate measures such as work training.

### Can I ask for a slower step-by-step return?

Yes, bring it up directly with the employer and the person who leads the plan. If you change your working time in relation to your sickness benefit, you must first contact Försäkringskassan, because it can affect your benefit.

### What happens if I cannot manage the return?

There are more steps in the process. Say so early, and the plan can be changed. Försäkringskassan assesses your ability to work and your right to benefit.

## What do I do now

Ask for a meeting where you go through where in the rehabilitation chain you are and what is assessed in the next step. Write down how the weeks feel during the step-by-step return. It is easier to see a pattern afterwards than in the moment, and it gives you something concrete to bring to the next review meeting. Read about the rehabilitation chain at [forsakringskassan.se](https://www.forsakringskassan.se).
