TITLE: Cover letter in easy Swedish: how to write one
SUMMARY: What is a cover letter and how do you write one? A simple guide in easy language with a short example.

A cover letter (personligt brev) is a short letter to the person who hires. You send it together with your CV. Here you get a simple guide in easy language: what you write, how you write it and an example.

Do you want to read a longer guide? Then there is [Cover letter: how to write a good application letter](/knowledge-base/article/personligt-brev).

## What is a cover letter?

A cover letter is also called an application letter.
It is a text where you tell about yourself.
You write why you want the job.
You also write why you fit.

The CV shows what you have done.
The letter shows who you are.

## What should I write?

Write five parts. Each part can be a few lines.

1. **Greeting.** Write "Hej" or "Hej [name]".
2. **Why you apply.** Write which job you are applying for.
3. **What you can do.** Write what you have done before.
4. **Why you fit.** Write why you are good for this job.
5. **End.** Write that you would like to come to an interview. Write your name.

## How do I do it?

Read the job ad first.
The ad says what the company wants.
Write about what you can do that fits.

Do not write the same thing as in the CV.
Write in your own way.
Write with simple words.

## Example of a short letter

"Hej,

Jag heter Anna och söker jobbet som butiksbiträde.

Jag har jobbat i butik förut.
Jag tycker om att prata med människor.
Jag kommer alltid i tid.

Jag tror att jag passar bra hos er.
Jag kommer gärna på intervju.

Vänliga hälsningar,
Anna"

## How long should the letter be?

The letter should be short.
It should fit on one page.
Read the letter again before you send it.
Ask someone to read it too. Then you can find spelling mistakes.

## What do the words mean?

- **CV:** a list of your jobs and your education.
- **Job ad (annons):** a text where the company tells that they are looking for someone.
- **Interview (intervju):** a meeting where you talk with the company.
