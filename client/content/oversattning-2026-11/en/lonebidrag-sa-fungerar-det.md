TITLE: Lönebidrag: how it works for you and the employer
SUMMARY: What lönebidrag is, who can get it, how the employer applies and what applies for pay and notice. How to bring it up with an employer.

Lönebidrag (wage subsidy for people with reduced work capacity) is financial support to the employer, so that the employer can adjust the job and the workplace to your work capacity. The money goes to the employer, not to you, and it is [Arbetsförmedlingen (the Swedish Public Employment Service)](https://arbetsformedlingen.se) that decides. Here is how lönebidrag works, what the employer needs to do and how you bring it up.

## What is lönebidrag

Lönebidrag is help for you if you are a job seeker, registered with Arbetsförmedlingen and have a disability that reduces your work capacity. The job can be with a private or public employer, permanent or for a limited time, full-time or part-time.

What decides is not a diagnosis. Arbetsförmedlingen looks at how your disability affects your work capacity in that particular job. The same person can need the support in one job and not in another.

## The three forms

| Form | For whom |
|---|---|
| Lönebidrag för utveckling i anställning (for development in employment) | You who need to develop your skills and work capacity, so that you can more easily get a job or start studying |
| Lönebidrag för anställning (for employment) | You who need support to get and keep a job that suits your skills |
| Lönebidrag för trygghet i anställning (for security in employment) | You who need long-term support to get and keep a job |

Arbetsförmedlingen assesses which form is relevant based on your situation. Read more about the last form in [Trygghetsanställning](/knowledge-base/article/trygghetsanstallning) and about the first in [Utvecklingsanställning](/knowledge-base/article/utvecklingsanstallning).

## How lönebidrag works in practice

You get your pay as usual from the employer. The subsidy is part of the employer's pay cost. What the employer gets and for how long is decided in Arbetsförmedlingen's decision. The first decision applies for a limited time and can be extended if you still need adjustment. The goal is that the job can continue in the long run without the support.

The subsidy belongs to that particular job. If you change employer, a new decision is needed for the new job.

## How the application works

It is the employer who applies, but you and your case officer lay the base.

1. You are registered with Arbetsförmedlingen and have talked to your case officer about what you need.
2. The employer sends a notice of interest to Arbetsförmedlingen.
3. Arbetsförmedlingen investigates together with you and the employer which adjustment and which support is needed, and makes a basis for the decision.
4. Arbetsförmedlingen makes a decision.
5. A few months after the job has started, Arbetsförmedlingen follows up.

**The job must not start before the decision has been made.** So it is not possible to apply for lönebidrag afterwards for a job you already have. Say this early to the employer so that you do not plan a start day that cannot be kept.

## What the employer needs to be able to show

This is good to know if an employer hesitates. Arbetsförmedlingen sets requirements, among other things, that the employer:

- is registered as an employer with Skatteverket (the Swedish Tax Agency) and reports tax and employer contributions
- offers pay and benefits according to kollektivavtal (collective agreement), or equivalent terms if there is no agreement
- pays out the whole pay electronically during the whole period
- has a good work environment and follows the decision about adjustments
- reports changes that can affect the subsidy, otherwise money may have to be paid back

The exact conditions are on Arbetsförmedlingen's pages for employers.

## Lönebidrag and notice period

Lönebidrag does not change your terms. You should have pay and benefits according to a kollektivavtal or equivalent terms. Your notice period therefore follows your employment contract and the agreement that applies at the employer, not the lönebidrag. Lönebidrag is a separate decision between the employer and Arbetsförmedlingen.

If the job is ended or something else in it changes, the employer must tell Arbetsförmedlingen, because it can affect the subsidy. If you are not sure what applies in your case: ask your case officer, the union or an employment law lawyer. General information about notice is in [Notice period and notice of termination](/knowledge-base/article/uppsagningstid-och-uppsagningsbesked).

## How to bring it up with an employer

You do not want lönebidrag to be the first thing an employer hears about you. Some ways to think:

- **Bring it up when the employer has shown interest.** The talk about terms is a natural place, before a contract is written and before a start day is decided.
- **Describe the adjustment, not the diagnosis.** "I need clear instructions in writing" says more than a label, and is all the employer needs to know.
- **Tell what it means for the employer.** You can say that the support is applied for at Arbetsförmedlingen, that they do the assessment and that the decision must exist before the start.
- **Let your case officer take part.** The case officer can contact the employer and explain the process.
- **Practice the wording in advance.** The [interview simulator](/interview-simulator) can help you practice what you want to say.

If you need help at the workplace itself, there is also [SIUS: support when you are introduced to a new job](/knowledge-base/article/sius-stod-vid-introduktion) and [Work aids at work](/knowledge-base/article/arbetshjalpmedel-pa-jobbet).

## Common questions

### How do I apply for lönebidrag?

You do not apply yourself. The employer sends a notice of interest, and Arbetsförmedlingen investigates with both of you. So the first step is a talk with your case officer.

### Can I employ someone with lönebidrag as an employer?

Yes, if a registered job seeker with reduced work capacity meets the conditions and you as an employer meet the requirements. Report your interest to Arbetsförmedlingen before the job starts. More is on arbetsformedlingen.se.

### Do I have to tell about my diagnosis?

To the employer it is enough that you describe which adjustments you need. At Arbetsförmedlingen it is different: there the case officer needs to understand your disability to be able to assess your need, and you will learn from your case officer which documents are required.

### Does lönebidrag affect my pay?

You have the right to pay and benefits according to the kollektivavtal, or equivalent terms if there is no agreement. Lönebidrag concerns what the employer gets as support, not what you should get as pay.

## What do I do now

Book a time with your case officer and describe as concretely as you can what makes some work tasks harder for you and which adjustment helps. That is the basis the whole assessment is built on. Current conditions are at [arbetsformedlingen.se](https://arbetsformedlingen.se).
