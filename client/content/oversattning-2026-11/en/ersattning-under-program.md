TITLE: Compensation in Rusta och matcha and other programs
SUMMARY: What compensation you get in a program, how to apply at Försäkringskassan and what reduces it. The difference from the aktivitetsrapport.

If you take part in a program at Arbetsförmedlingen (the Swedish Public Employment Service), for example Rusta och matcha (a program that helps you prepare for work and find a job), the compensation is aktivitetsstöd (activity support) or utvecklingsersättning (development allowance). [Försäkringskassan](https://www.forsakringskassan.se) (the Swedish Social Insurance Agency) pays it out, and you apply there yourself. [Arbetsförmedlingen](https://arbetsformedlingen.se) is responsible for the program, approves absence and decides on any suspension from the compensation. The details about the compensation itself are in [Aktivitetsstöd: how it works](/knowledge-base/article/aktivitetsstod-sa-fungerar-det).

## Two agencies, one measure

When you have been referred to a program, it is easy to think that one agency handles the whole chain. That is not so. They do different parts, and you need to keep track of both.

| Question | Who answers |
|---|---|
| Can I take part in the program, and what should I do there? | Arbetsförmedlingen |
| Can I be away for a day? | Arbetsförmedlingen |
| Do I get compensation, and how is it worked out? | Försäkringskassan |
| Application and payment | Försäkringskassan |
| Will I be left without compensation because I missed something? | Arbetsförmedlingen |

## Rusta och matcha compensation: which compensation do I get

If you take part in Rusta och matcha or another program, you can apply for compensation at Försäkringskassan. Which form it is depends on your situation:

- **Aktivitetsstöd with an income-based amount**, when you meet the conditions that are based on a-kassa's income information.
- **Aktivitetsstöd with a fixed amount**, for you who are older and do not meet the conditions for the income-based one.
- **Utvecklingsersättning**, for you who are younger and do not meet the conditions for the income-based one.

Försäkringskassan decides which form applies to you. Amounts and age limits are on their website.

## How to apply

You apply at Försäkringskassan afterwards, the month after the program. You do this in the e-service on Mina sidor (My pages) or on a form. In the application you state which days you took part and which days you were absent, and why. Försäkringskassan states at the start of the month an application window that gives payment the same month. If you miss it, the money comes later.

You submit one application at a time.

## Two logins, two reports

What causes trouble is that it feels like one matter but is handled in two places:

- At Arbetsförmedlingen you submit your **aktivitetsrapport** (activity report). See [Activity report: how to do it](/knowledge-base/article/aktivitetsrapport-guide).
- At Försäkringskassan you apply for the **compensation**.

You can have done right at one and still not get money, because you missed the other. If the payment does not come, it is Försäkringskassan you contact.

## Sickness and absence

Report sickness to Arbetsförmedlingen on the first day of sickness. The first day of sickness is usually a karensdag (a qualifying day without compensation) according to Försäkringskassan. If you care for a sick child, you keep the compensation if you cannot take part. Other approved forms of absence, for example a break, usually need approval from Arbetsförmedlingen in advance. Ask before. Trips abroad also need to be approved in advance, otherwise the compensation can be lost. See [Travelling away when unemployed](/knowledge-base/article/resa-bort-som-arbetslos).

## What can reduce the compensation

Försäkringskassan deducts some things:

- **Pay** for a day when you also take part in the program. Ob-tillägg (extra pay for unsocial hours) and commission also count as pay.
- Some employment benefits, for example severance pay.
- Parental benefit, sickness and activity compensation, student support and pension can reduce or exclude the compensation.

If you start working, read [Compensation when you start working](/knowledge-base/article/ersattning-nar-du-borjar-jobba).

## If you miss something

Arbetsförmedlingen can decide that you are left without compensation. Since 1 June 2026, stricter rules apply for program participants: among other things, you can be left without compensation directly for unauthorized absence. After repeated decisions, the aktivitetsstöd can also be lowered. Read [Warning from Arbetsförmedlingen](/knowledge-base/article/varning-fran-arbetsformedlingen).

## And a-kassa?

Aktivitetsstöd with an income-based amount is worked out using a-kassa's income information. How your a-kassa is treated while you are in the program depends on your case, so ask your a-kassa before the program starts and after it ends.

## What do I do now

If you have been referred to a program: apply for compensation at Försäkringskassan the month after, and submit your aktivitetsrapport at Arbetsförmedlingen. If you are already under way and unsure: log in at Försäkringskassan and check the status of your application instead of assuming that it is handled automatically.

## Common questions

### Do I get compensation in Rusta och matcha?

You can apply for compensation at Försäkringskassan. You get it if you meet the conditions and take part according to the program.

### Who pays aktivitetsstöd?

Försäkringskassan. Arbetsförmedlingen does not pay out anything itself.

### What is the difference between aktivitetsstöd and utvecklingsersättning?

They are different forms of compensation in programs, depending on, among other things, age and whether you meet the conditions for the income-based one. Försäkringskassan decides which one you get.

### Do I get compensation if I am sick during the program?

Report the sickness to Arbetsförmedlingen on the first day. The first day of sickness is usually a karensdag. When you need a doctor's certificate is on Försäkringskassan's website.
