TITLE: Nystartsjobb: rules, application and extension
SUMMARY: How nystartsjobb (new start job) works for you and the employer: who can get it, how the application works, extension and what to say to an employer.

Nystartsjobb (new start job) is a support where the employer who hires you gets money towards your pay, because you have been away from working life for some time or are newly arrived in Sweden. The employer applies to [Arbetsförmedlingen](https://arbetsformedlingen.se) (the Swedish Public Employment Service), not you, and the job must not start before Arbetsförmedlingen has made a decision.

The rules and time periods for nystartsjobb change often. Therefore no amounts, age limits or numbers of months are given here. You can see exactly what applies to you on Arbetsförmedlingen's page about nystartsjobb, or you can ask your case officer (handläggare).

## What is a nystartsjobb

It is a normal employment, permanent or for a limited time, full-time or part-time. The support goes to the employer for a period. The pay must follow the employer's kollektivavtal (collective agreement), or be equal to it if there is no agreement. You have employment protection under lagen om anställningsskydd (the Employment Protection Act), even if some parts can be agreed differently in a collective agreement.

At the workplace you are a normal employee. The support is a matter between the employer and Arbetsförmedlingen.

## Who can get nystartsjobb

It is about how long you have been away from working life within a certain time frame. What can count is time when you:

- have been unemployed and registered at Arbetsförmedlingen, or have taken part in a labor market program
- have received sjukpenning (sickness benefit), rehabiliteringspenning (rehabilitation benefit), sjukersättning (sickness compensation) or aktivitetsersättning (activity compensation)
- have received försörjningsstöd (financial support from the municipality) while you were unemployed

The requirements for how long and within which time frame are different for younger and older people. Other groups have their own grounds, for example people who take part in etableringsprogrammet (the establishment program) or in jobb- och utvecklingsgarantin (the job and development guarantee). See [Etableringsprogrammet](/knowledge-base/article/etableringsprogrammet) and [Jobb- och utvecklingsgarantin](/knowledge-base/article/jobb-och-utvecklingsgarantin).

You can do an anonymous test on Arbetsförmedlingen's website that gives a first indication. The final decision is made by an arbetsförmedlare (a case officer at Arbetsförmedlingen). You need to be registered at Arbetsförmedlingen when the decision is made. If you have deregistered, you need to register again. How: [Skriva in dig på Arbetsförmedlingen](/knowledge-base/article/skriva-in-dig-arbetsformedlingen) (registering at Arbetsförmedlingen).

## How the application works

The employer makes the application for nystartsjobb. Arbetsförmedlingen describes the steps like this:

1. **You** find a job, for example in Platsbanken (Arbetsförmedlingen's job board), where the employer has marked that nystartsjobb is an advantage. The employer can also choose a person they have already found.
2. **The employer** sends an application to Arbetsförmedlingen. This is done digitally with e-legitimation (electronic ID), or with a form.
3. **You** get an email or text message about how to log in to Mina sidor (My pages). There you answer questions about your time outside working life. The answers are the basis for the decision.
4. **Arbetsförmedlingen** decides if the employer gets nystartsjobb for the employment.

The employment must not start before the decision. Arbetsförmedlingen writes that the application must be made a certain time before the employment starts and that the processing times are currently longer than usual. Therefore tell the employer early that the start day cannot be decided until the decision is there.

## Applying for nystartsjobb as an employer

This is good to know if you talk to an employer. The employer must, among other things:

- be registered at Skatteverket (the Swedish Tax Agency)
- pay the whole salary electronically to your account
- follow a collective agreement or equal terms
- not hire you out to someone else
- meet certain conditions about debts and business bans
- report changes that can affect the support

Nystartsjobb can also be applied for through an expression of interest to Arbetsförmedlingen, or through an ad in Platsbanken. The conditions are on Arbetsförmedlingen's pages for employers.

## Extension of nystartsjobb

The nystartsjobb is valid for a limited time. If the employer wants to extend, they apply for an extension, with e-legitimation or a form. Arbetsförmedlingen then investigates if it is possible to get more time. The extension must be applied for in good time before the current period ends, and how long the support can be extended depends on your situation. The employment itself does not have to end when the support ends.

## How to mention nystartsjobb to an employer

You do not need to know the rules by heart. A few sentences are enough:

> "There is a support called nystartsjobb. You as the employer apply for it at Arbetsförmedlingen, and it is decided before I start. I have talked to my case officer about whether I can meet the conditions."

Do not bring up the support as the first thing you say. It works best when the employer already thinks you seem suitable. If you want to be prepared, [Berätta om dig själv](/knowledge-base/article/beratta-om-dig-sjalv) (tell about yourself) can help you shape the first impression.

## Other supports that may be relevant

If nystartsjobb does not fit, there are others. See [Lönebidrag](/knowledge-base/article/lonebidrag-sa-fungerar-det) (wage subsidy) if you have a disability, [Introduktionsjobb](/knowledge-base/article/introduktionsjobb) (introduction job) and [Etableringsjobb](/knowledge-base/article/etableringsjobb) (establishment job). ## Common questions

### Can I apply for nystartsjobb myself?

No, the employer applies. You add information in Mina sidor after the application has come in.

### What is nystartsjobb and anställningsstöd?

Nystartsjobb is one of several anställningsstöd (employment supports). Arbetsförmedlingen has several, and which one fits depends on your situation. Ask your case officer.

### What counts as time outside working life?

Registered unemployment, programs, sick leave with certain benefits, and försörjningsstöd together with unemployment. Arbetsförmedlingen decides how your time is counted.

### How long does the application take?

The processing times vary and are currently longer than usual according to Arbetsförmedlingen. Apply in good time.

## What do I do now

Ask your case officer directly: "Do I qualify for nystartsjobb, and if so, for how long?" You can bring the answer with you to every talk with an employer. Read more at [arbetsformedlingen.se](https://arbetsformedlingen.se).
