TITLE: Managing stress: advice from 1177 for job seekers
SUMMARY: What stress is, signs that stress makes you feel bad, what you can do yourself according to 1177, and when to seek health care.

Stress management is what you can do so that the stress eases and your body can rest. According to [1177](https://www.1177.se) (the Swedish health care advice service), stress can become harmful if it goes on for too long, so you also need time for recovery and sleep. Looking for a job has many things that cause stress: waiting, rejections, money worries and not controlling the result yourself. Feeling bad because of that is a reasonable reaction, not a sign that you are doing something wrong.

This page is based on advice from 1177 and is not medical advice. If you feel bad, read the section "When to seek health care" further down.

## What is stress?

According to 1177, stress is a reaction in the body that gives you extra strength for a while: the heart beats faster, breathing increases and the muscles tense. This is helpful for a short time. The brain and the body cannot handle unlimited stress, and need rest to work.

## Signs that stress is making you feel bad

1177 mentions, among other things:

- difficulty relaxing or falling asleep, or waking up too early
- more difficulty concentrating and a worse memory
- irritation, worry or anxiety
- stomach ache or headache, heart palpitations and tense muscles
- low mood and avoiding friends
- using alcohol, nicotine, caffeine or sleeping pills to manage

If you recognize yourself in several of them, it is a reason to take it seriously, not to feel ashamed.

## What can I do myself?

1177 lists, among other things, these ways to reduce stress. You do not need to do everything. Choose what feels possible today.

- **Move regularly.** Physical activity helps against stress.
- **Take breaks for recovery.** Rest, do something you enjoy, be in nature, take a walk, be with people who make you feel good.
- **Write two lists:** one of what stresses you and one of what makes you feel good. Use them to do more of the second.
- **Put your thoughts into words.** Talking to someone or writing it down can make it clearer what you worry about.
- **Practice setting limits.** You can say no or ask for time to think.
- **Plan recovery** in your calendar, and think about whether you demand too much of yourself.
- **Put away the phone** and social media for a while.
- **Keep regular routines** for sleep and food.
- **Slow down.** Use breathing breaks or relaxation exercises when you are stressed. Mindfulness or relaxation can be good, especially before bedtime.
- **Be careful with alcohol.** It can feel calming but can make you more sensitive to stress.
- **Keep in touch with family and friends**, and avoid isolating yourself.

More about mindfulness for job seekers is in [Mindfulness och stresshantering för jobbsökare](/knowledge-base/article/mindfulness-stresshantering-jobbsokare) (mindfulness and stress management for job seekers).

## Stress in the job search

Some things that belong to the job search itself, and that are not medical:

- Decide on a time when you stop searching for the day, so that the job search is not going on in the back of your mind all day and night. [Struktur i jobbsökningen](/knowledge-base/article/struktur-jobbsokning) (structure in the job search) shows how.
- Divide big tasks, for example an application, into small steps.
- Write down each job you apply for on the same day, so that you do not have to worry that you forgot something.
- Do you put off applications and then feel guilty? Start with something small, not with everything.
- If you have a case officer or a work consultant (arbetskonsulent): tell them that you are under pressure. It is easier to adjust the plan if they know.

The portal's tools [Mående och energi](/wellness) (mood and energy) and [Dagbok](/diary) (diary) can help you see what takes and gives energy. More texts: [Hantera stress under jobbsökningen](/knowledge-base/article/stresshantering-jobbsokning) (handling stress during the job search), [Mental hälsa under jobbsökningen](/knowledge-base/article/mental-halsa-guide) (mental health during the job search).

## When to seek health care

According to 1177, you can contact a vårdcentral (health center) or företagshälsovården (occupational health care) if you have high stress and get sleep problems, anxiety or heart palpitations. Seek help as soon as possible if stress makes you feel bad and you cannot handle it yourself, because stress-related illnesses can take a long time to heal. Psychotherapy, for example CBT (cognitive behavioral therapy) online, can help.

If you are not sure where to turn: call **1177** for health care advice. You do not need to be sure that it is stress.

## If things are very hard

If you have thoughts of taking your life or hurting yourself, do not wait. Call **112** if there is immediate danger. You can also contact **Mind Självmordslinjen** (Mind suicide line) by phone on **90101** or by chat on mind.se. You can also call 1177.

## Common questions

### What is stress management?

What you do to reduce and handle stress and to recover, for example rest, movement, routines for sleep and food, setting limits and talking to someone.

### Does it help to breathe calmly?

1177 mentions breathing breaks and relaxation exercises as something you can use when you are stressed.

### Is it dangerous to be stressed?

Stress is a reaction in the body that can be helpful for a while. According to 1177, stress can become harmful if it goes on for too long. Then it is time to seek help.

## What do I do now

Choose one thing from the list above and do it today, for example a walk or writing down what stresses you. If you feel bad and notice that your sleep, worry or body do not calm down, call 1177.
