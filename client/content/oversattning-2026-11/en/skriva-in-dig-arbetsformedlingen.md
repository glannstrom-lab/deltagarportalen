TITLE: Registering with Arbetsförmedlingen: how to do it
SUMMARY: When to register, how to do it with and without e-legitimation, what happens after registering, and what to bring to the planning meeting.

You register with Arbetsförmedlingen on the website of [Arbetsförmedlingen (the Swedish Public Employment Service)](https://arbetsformedlingen.se), with e-legitimation (electronic ID), on the first day you are unemployed. After you register you get a time for a planning meeting, where you and a case officer make an action plan. Here is how it works and why the day matters.

## When you should register

Register on the first day you are unemployed. You can start to fill in the information before, but wait to send it in until the day has come.

It matters for two reasons that Arbetsförmedlingen itself mentions. Your a-kassa (unemployment insurance fund) assesses your right to benefit from the day you registered. And to keep your sjukpenninggrundande inkomst, SGI (the income your sickness benefit is based on), at [Försäkringskassan (the Swedish Social Insurance Agency)](https://www.forsakringskassan.se) you need to be registered. So if you register later, it is not only a formality that is delayed.

If you have received a varsel (warning of redundancy), you do not need to register because of that. Read more in [Varsel: what it means](/knowledge-base/article/varsel-vad-betyder-det).

## How to register, step by step

1. Go to "Skriv in dig" on Arbetsförmedlingen's website and log in with e-legitimation.
2. Fill in information about education, work experience and what kind of job you are looking for.
3. Send it in. You get a time for your first meeting.
4. Apply for benefit at your a-kassa. Registering does not replace the application to the a-kassa. If you are not a member, there is more in [How the a-kassa works](/knowledge-base/article/a-kassa-sa-fungerar-det).

### Without e-legitimation

If you do not have e-legitimation, you can visit a servicekontor (service office). There an employee can help you log in with a one-time code. You need to show ID and have a mobile phone. If you live far from a service office, you can call Arbetsförmedlingen, but Arbetsförmedlingen writes that the registration is complete only when you have shown ID at a service office. Read more in [Digital services you need](/knowledge-base/article/digitala-tjanster-du-behover).

## The planning meeting and the action plan

At the planning meeting you meet a case officer, digitally or in person. You go through your background and what you are aiming for, and make a shared action plan. It describes what you will do to get closer to a job and which support may be relevant. If you have a job coming up, you do not need a planning meeting according to Arbetsförmedlingen. How close in time the job must be, you can see on Arbetsförmedlingen's page.

The action plan changes when your situation changes. More about what happens during the meeting is in [Your planning at Arbetsförmedlingen](/knowledge-base/article/din-planering-hos-arbetsformedlingen).

Do you want to change case officer if it does not work? That has its own guide: [Changing case officer at Arbetsförmedlingen](/knowledge-base/article/byta-handlaggare-arbetsformedlingen).

## Being registered is not the same as taking part in a program

Being registered means that you are registered as a job seeker and have an action plan. Taking part in a program, for example [Rusta och matcha](/knowledge-base/article/rusta-och-matcha) (a program for job seekers who need support), is an extra decision that Arbetsförmedlingen makes based on your needs. You can be registered without taking part in any program, but you cannot take part in a program without being registered. Arbetsförmedlingen assesses how you become relevant for Rusta och matcha at a follow-up meeting.

## After registering

Being registered is a status you keep alive:

- Every month you report your activities in the activity report (aktivitetsrapport). See [Activity report: how to do it](/knowledge-base/article/aktivitetsrapport-guide).
- You may be offered measures or programs if your case officer assesses that you need it.
- If your phone number or email changes, you tell this through Mina sidor ("My pages"), by phone or at a service office. You do not need to report address changes, because Arbetsförmedlingen gets it from Skatteverket (the Swedish Tax Agency).

## How to prepare for the first meeting

- Your CV, even if it is not finished. Build one in the [CV builder](/cv) if you do not have one.
- A list of education and earlier jobs, with approximate times.
- What you want to work with, or which fields you are open to.
- If you have a disability or a health condition that affects which support you need. It decides which support may be relevant.
- Questions, written down so that you do not forget them.

## Deregistering

If you get a job, go on parental leave or start studying, you deregister through Mina sidor with "Skriv ut mig". Also tell your a-kassa. Read [Benefit when you start working](/knowledge-base/article/ersattning-nar-du-borjar-jobba) before you say yes, if you have benefit.

## What do I do now

If you are unemployed today and not registered: register now. If you already have a time for the planning meeting: collect the points in the list above before you go there.

## Common questions

### Do I have to register before I have a new job?

It depends on which benefit you want. The a-kassa assesses your right from the day you registered, and SGI at Försäkringskassan requires that you are registered. If you ask your a-kassa, you will know what applies to you.

### What happens if I miss the planning meeting?

Contact Arbetsförmedlingen as soon as you can and book a new time. Whether a missed meeting can affect your benefit is decided by Arbetsförmedlingen and your a-kassa. Read [Warning from Arbetsförmedlingen](/knowledge-base/article/varning-fran-arbetsformedlingen).

### How do I log in to Mina sidor?

With e-legitimation. Without e-legitimation you can get a one-time code from an employee at a service office.

### Who does not need to register?

If you have received a varsel, you do not need to register just because of that. For other situations: ask Arbetsförmedlingen or your a-kassa.
