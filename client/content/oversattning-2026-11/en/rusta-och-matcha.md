TITLE: Rusta och matcha – what is it and how does it work?
SUMMARY: What is included in Rusta och matcha, who can get it, how long it lasts and who decides. Links to how you choose and change provider.

Rusta och matcha (a matching service run by Arbetsförmedlingen) is a support from Arbetsförmedlingen for you who have been registered for a long time. You choose a provider yourself who supports you toward a job or education, and you get a supervisor there. It is [Arbetsförmedlingen (the Swedish Public Employment Service)](https://arbetsformedlingen.se) that decides if you are offered Rusta och matcha. Here is what the service is and what is included.

## What is Rusta och matcha

Rusta och matcha is a matching service. Instead of Arbetsförmedlingen coaching you itself, you go to an independent provider that is approved by Arbetsförmedlingen for the task. The provider can be a company or an organization near where you live.

The service is mainly for people who have been registered for a long time. You must meet certain requirements and be part of the program förmedlingsinsatser (placement measures). Arbetsförmedlingen assesses at a follow-up meeting if you should be assigned, with the help of a statistical assessment tool. This means you cannot sign up yourself, but you can bring it up with your case officer.

## What is included

According to Arbetsförmedlingen, you get a supervisor at the provider who makes activities together with you, for example:

- contacts with employers
- job search
- information about education
- guidance

You have regular individual meetings with the supervisor and also meetings on site at the provider. How often is stated in Arbetsförmedlingen's assignment, and you can ask the provider to show it to you. The support can be combined with sfi (Swedish for immigrants), and interpreter and language support are available. It can also be a writing interpreter or a sign language interpreter.

## How long it lasts

You take part for a period, and after that Arbetsförmedlingen evaluates. If you are assessed to need more time, you can be offered another period. The length is stated in your decision.

## You choose the provider and can change

You choose the provider yourself. If you do not choose, Arbetsförmedlingen appoints one for you, near where you live. You compare, among other things, ratings and distance. How to make the choice, and what to compare, is in [How to choose a provider in Rusta och matcha](/knowledge-base/article/valja-leverantor-rusta-och-matcha).

If it does not work, you can change by contacting Arbetsförmedlingen. First bring up what is wrong with the provider, and then give your views to Arbetsförmedlingen if needed. How the change works is described in [Changing provider in Rusta och matcha](/knowledge-base/article/byta-leverantor-rusta-och-matcha). This page does not recommend any single provider.

## Who decides what

| Question | Who |
|---|---|
| Do I get Rusta och matcha? | Arbetsförmedlingen |
| Who gives me the support? | You choose the provider |
| Compensation in the meantime | Försäkringskassan (the Swedish Social Insurance Agency) pays, Arbetsförmedlingen decides on suspension |
| My status as a job seeker | Arbetsförmedlingen, and your a-kassa (unemployment insurance fund) if you have a-kassa |

The provider is paid by Arbetsförmedlingen, and part of the payment depends on you getting a job or education. Therefore you may need to give a certificate from the employer. The payment to the provider comes from Arbetsförmedlingen, not from you.

## Compensation in the meantime

You take part in a program and can apply for compensation at Försäkringskassan. How it works is in [Aktivitetsstöd – how it works](/knowledge-base/article/aktivitetsstod-sa-fungerar-det) and [Compensation during programs](/knowledge-base/article/ersattning-under-program). If you do not show up, according to Arbetsförmedlingen you can be without compensation, or lose your place in the program. Read [Warning from Arbetsförmedlingen](/knowledge-base/article/varning-fran-arbetsformedlingen) to see how such decisions are made.

## Rusta och matcha does not replace the activity report

You are still a job seeker at Arbetsförmedlingen. Even if the provider helps you, you send in the activity report yourself. See [Activity report – how to do it](/knowledge-base/article/aktivitetsrapport-guide).

## What do I do now

If you have been offered Rusta och matcha: read how to choose a provider, and tell your choice on Mina sidor (My pages) or to Arbetsförmedlingen. If you think it could suit you but nobody has mentioned it: bring it up at your next meeting with your case officer.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Rusta och matcha?

A support where you get a supervisor at a provider that you have chosen yourself, to get closer to a job or education.

### What is included in Rusta och matcha?

Activities made with your supervisor, for example contacts with employers, job search and guidance, and regular meetings.

### How does Rusta och matcha work if I already have a-kassa?

You are still registered and should report your activities. How the compensation is affected during the program time depends on your situation. Ask your a-kassa and read [Compensation during programs](/knowledge-base/article/ersattning-under-program).

### Can I say no?

Arbetsförmedlingen decides that, and if you do not show up it can affect both compensation and your place. Talk to your case officer before you say no.

### Does it cost me anything?

The provider gets its payment from Arbetsförmedlingen, not from you. If you are unsure about something that concerns money, ask your case officer.
