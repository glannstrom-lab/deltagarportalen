TITLE: Arbetspraktik via Arbetsförmedlingen – and compensation
SUMMARY: How arbetspraktik works, who can get it, and what to ask about compensation at Arbetsförmedlingen and Försäkringskassan before you start.

Arbetspraktik (work placement) is when you try working at a workplace to get work experience, through [Arbetsförmedlingen (the Swedish Public Employment Service)](https://arbetsformedlingen.se). During the placement you take part in a program and can apply for compensation at [Försäkringskassan (the Swedish Social Insurance Agency)](https://www.forsakringskassan.se). What applies to you in particular depends on your situation, so ask your case officer before you say yes.

## What is arbetspraktik?

According to Arbetsförmedlingen, you try working at a workplace to get work experience and strengthen your professional skills. It can also be a way to prepare if you want to start your own business. The placement can be at a company, an organization or an authority.

This is how it goes:

1. Arbetsförmedlingen first assesses if arbetspraktik suits you.
2. You find a placement yourself or with help from Arbetsförmedlingen.
3. The set-up is planned together by you, the employer and Arbetsförmedlingen, which also contacts the union.
4. After the placement you get a certificate from the workplace about your experience.

If you are newly arrived with a vocational education, your professional skills can also be assessed during the placement.

## Who can get arbetspraktik?

You must be registered as a job seeker at Arbetsförmedlingen. In addition there are conditions that concern your age and your situation: that you are unemployed or at risk of becoming so, are far from the labour market, take part in one of the guarantees or in the etableringsprogrammet (establishment programme), or have a disability that affects your work ability. Exactly which conditions apply and if you meet them is decided by Arbetsförmedlingen. If you are not registered: [Registering with Arbetsförmedlingen](/knowledge-base/article/skriva-in-dig-arbetsformedlingen).

## Arbetspraktik and compensation

This is the question that is most important to get right.

- Arbetsförmedlingen writes that during arbetspraktik you take part in a program and can apply for compensation at Försäkringskassan.
- Försäkringskassan writes that a person who takes part in Arbetsförmedlingen's programs can get aktivitetsstöd (activity support) or utvecklingsersättning (development compensation), and that newly arrived people in the etableringsprogrammet get etableringsersättning (establishment compensation).
- Which compensation you have today matters for what applies. Read [Compensation in Rusta och matcha and other programs](/knowledge-base/article/ersattning-under-program) and [Aktivitetsstöd – what it is and how to apply](/knowledge-base/article/aktivitetsstod-sa-fungerar-det).

If you have a-kassa (unemployment insurance fund), sjukpenning (sickness benefit) or other compensation, ask the case officer and the body that pays your compensation before you start. A decision that seems reasonable can affect your compensation in a way you did not expect.

## Arbetspraktik, volunteer placement and placement through school

| Type | How it works |
|---|---|
| Arbetspraktik through Arbetsförmedlingen | Planned with Arbetsförmedlingen. Compensation is applied for at Försäkringskassan. |
| Placement in an education | Belongs to the education. Contact the school. |
| Volunteer work | At non-profit organizations. You get experience and references. If you have compensation, ask before. |
| Placement you arrange completely yourself | You contact companies directly. Ask your case officer or the body that pays your compensation what applies. |

You can compare with [Arbetsträning](/knowledge-base/article/arbetstraning-vad-innebar-det) and [Trial work without pay](/knowledge-base/article/provjobba-utan-lon).

## How to find a placement

- Tell your case officer which job you want to try, and ask for help.
- Contact employers directly. The portal's [spontaneous application](/spontanansökan) helps you find companies.
- Ask in your network. See [Networking – your hidden job market](/knowledge-base/article/natverka-for-jobb).
- Ask the school's study counsellor if it belongs to an education.

## How to ask a company

Keep the e-mail short. Write who you are, which job you want to try, how long and which days you can, and that Arbetsförmedlingen plans the placement together with them. Call afterwards if you like. Remember that the employer should know what the placement means for them in practice: who guides you and which tasks you get.

## During the placement

- Ask questions and be curious. Offer to help.
- Write down every week which tasks you did and which systems you used. This becomes material for your CV and interview answers.
- Report sick according to the rules that apply to your program. Ask the case officer how to do it, so that you do not lose compensation for sick days.
- Ask for a closing talk, a reference and a certificate before you leave. Save your supervisor's contact details.

## If the placement is not what you expected

A placement is there for you to learn and try a job, not to replace employed staff. If you learn nothing, get no guidance or feel that you are being used: bring it up with your supervisor and your case officer at Arbetsförmedlingen. You do not have to put up with it.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I get pay during arbetspraktik?

During arbetspraktik you take part in a program, and Arbetsförmedlingen writes that you can apply for compensation at Försäkringskassan. Ask your case officer what applies to you and if the placement should pay anything.

### What is the difference between arbetspraktik and arbetsträning?

Arbetspraktik is a support where you try a workplace. Arbetsträning is another measure with its own conditions. Read [Arbetsträning](/knowledge-base/article/arbetstraning-vad-innebar-det).

### Can the placement lead to a job?

It can, but nothing is guaranteed. Bring it up about halfway: ask what you need to show, and ask for a reference.

## What do I do now

Decide which job you want to try. Book or call your case officer at Arbetsförmedlingen and ask about arbetspraktik, and what it means for your compensation. Also ask your a-kassa or Försäkringskassan if you get compensation from one of them.
