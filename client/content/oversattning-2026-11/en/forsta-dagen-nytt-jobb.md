TITLE: First day at a new job – tips for new employees
SUMMARY: What to ask and prepare before, how the first day can go, and how to settle in during your first week and month at a new job.

Are you new at a job and wondering what will happen on the first day? The most important thing is to know where you should be, who to ask for and what you need to bring. The rest comes with time: nobody expects you to know everything on the first day. Here is a guide from the days before to the first month, which works even if it is your first job.

## Before the first day

### Ask and check

You can send a short e-mail or message before:

- What time should I be there, and who do I ask for?
- How do I get in: reception, gate, access card or a colleague who meets me?
- Do I need to bring anything, for example ID, work clothes or details for the pay?
- Is there a plan for the first day?

Get in touch in advance if you have needs that the employer should know about, such as an adjustment of the workplace. See [Adjustments at the workplace](/knowledge-base/article/anpassningar-arbetsplats).

### Practical things

- Test the route in advance so you know how long it takes.
- Put out clothes and bag the evening before.
- Bring something to write with, and lunch if you do not know how it works.

## The first day

### When you arrive

Introduce yourself to the person you should ask for. If you have been given a way to check in, pass through or register arrival, ask how it works. It is good to ask a first question: "What should I do first?"

### During the introduction

- **Take notes.** Names of colleagues, routines, systems and questions that come up. It can help to draw a simple map of who sits where.
- **Ask.** Good questions are "How does this work?", "Who do I ask about that?" and "What is expected of me in the first week?"
- **Passwords and logins:** ask how you handle them at the workplace, instead of saving them in a place that others can reach.
- **Get to know your colleagues.** Ask about their roles. Say yes to fika (a coffee break) or lunch if you have the energy.

### When the day is over

Write down what you learned and what you want to ask tomorrow. It is reasonable to be tired after a first day.

## The first week

- Book short meetings with the people you will work with.
- Learn the recurring meetings, deadlines and how the team talks to each other: e-mail, chat or meetings.
- Ask your manager what is most important to have ready first.
- Arrange your workspace so that you can work there.

## The first month

- Deliver what you promise and say early if you do not have time.
- Ask for feedback: "How do you think it is going so far?"
- Do not take on too much too fast. Basics first.
- It is okay not to know everything.

If you want a plan for the whole time, see [30-60-90 day plan](/knowledge-base/article/forsta-90-dagarna).

## Is it your first job?

Everybody starts somewhere. You do not need to know all the routines of the job, but it helps to ask questions, to say when something is unclear and to ask for help. Get your employment contract in writing and read it: [How to read an employment contract](/knowledge-base/article/las-anstallningsavtal). If you are looking for your first job, there is [First job after studies](/knowledge-base/article/forsta-jobbet-efter-studier).

## Common mistakes

- Comparing with the last job: "At my last job we did it like this..."
- Being in too much of a hurry to change things.
- Being too quiet. Ask and show that you want to learn.
- Eating lunch alone every day, if you want to get to know people.
- Ignoring how the workplace works before you have understood why.

## What do I do now

Send a short e-mail with the questions above. Do the exercise "Preparing for the first day" among the exercises in Jobin, so you prepare in a practical and mental way. Then get in touch if something is unclear, before it becomes a problem.

## Frequently asked questions

### What should I bring on the first day at a new job?

Ask the employer, because it differs. It can be something to write with, ID if the employer asks for it, and lunch if you do not know how it works.

### How should I be as a new employee?

Curious and honest about what you do not know. Ask, take notes and listen.

### What do I do if I get nervous?

Prepare the practical things in advance, then you have less to think about. You can also tell your manager that this is new for you.

### What do I do if it does not work at the new job?

Bring it up with your manager early, preferably as soon as it has become clear. The terms of your employment, for example the trial period, are in [How probationary employment works](/knowledge-base/article/provanstallning-guide).
