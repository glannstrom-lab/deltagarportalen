TITLE: Activity report – how activity reporting works
SUMMARY: What to write in the activity report, how to send it with or without e-legitimation (electronic ID), and what to do if you miss the time window.

Activity reporting (aktivitetsrapportering) works like this: once a month, within a time window that Arbetsförmedlingen (the Swedish Public Employment Service) states, you send in a report about what you have done during the month that has passed. You do it on Mina sidor (My pages) with e-legitimation (electronic ID), and the report is the basis for your compensation. Here is what to include, how to send it and what to do if something goes wrong.

## What the activity report is

The activity report (aktivitetsrapport) is your report to [Arbetsförmedlingen](https://arbetsformedlingen.se) about what you have done to get closer to a job. You must send it if you are unemployed and registered. This also applies when you do a work placement (praktik) or work training (arbetsträning).

The report is not something you fill in just for the sake of it. It is the basis for deciding if you meet the conditions for your compensation, from your a-kassa (unemployment insurance fund) or in a program, and Arbetsförmedlingen checks it. If you do not report, or report late, you can get a warning or be without compensation for a number of days.

## What to include

Write what you have really done, not what you plan to do. Arbetsförmedlingen says the report should include:

- all jobs you have applied for, both advertised jobs and jobs that are not advertised, for example a spontaneous application, and jobs you got a tip about or were told to apply for
- work you have had
- recruitment meetings and job interviews
- studies you have taken part in

You should not write illness in the report. If you get sick, Arbetsförmedlingen has a separate way for that, see [Sick when you are unemployed](/knowledge-base/article/sjuk-nar-du-ar-arbetslos).

### How to describe an activity

Write so that another person can follow what you did without asking you. Three things are enough:

- **What:** applied for a job, went to an interview, took a course.
- **Where:** which company or which education.
- **When:** which date.

Also save the proof, for example applications, answers from employers and screenshots. Arbetsförmedlingen makes checks and states how long you should keep it. You can find the time on their page about activity reporting.

## When you send it in

The report is sent every month within a window at the beginning of the month, and it covers the month before. You can see the dates on Mina sidor and on Arbetsförmedlingen's page about activity reporting. Put the window in your calendar. A window is not a recommendation, and if you do not send it in within the window, it counts as if the report is missing.

## How to send it in

### With e-legitimation

Log in to Mina sidor and choose to report your activities. There you can add activities during the month, send in the report and see earlier reports. This way you do not have to remember everything at the last minute.

### Without e-legitimation

You have three ways:

- Log in to Mina sidor with a one-time code at a servicekontor (service office). You need to show ID and have a mobile phone.
- Fill in the form, print it and send it by post or hand it in at a servicekontor. It is not possible to send the form by e-mail.
- Report orally by phone.

Post it in good time, so that the form arrives within the window.

## What happens after you have sent it in

First there is an automatic check. A case officer can also check the report manually, for example if there are few jobs applied for, if the same job is included as before, or if you have filled in a free-text field. On Mina sidor you can see when the report has been checked.

## If you miss the window or something goes wrong

Arbetsförmedlingen writes that you can get a warning or be without compensation for one or more days if you do not send in the report in time or in some other way do not take care of your job search. If you get compensation from an a-kassa, the a-kassa decides, after Arbetsförmedlingen has told them. If you are in a program, Arbetsförmedlingen decides itself. Read how this works in [Warning from Arbetsförmedlingen](/knowledge-base/article/varning-fran-arbetsformedlingen).

If you have a reason, for example a technical error or illness, get in touch as soon as you can and preferably before the deadline. If you save screenshots of the error, you have proof. If the reason is enough, the authorities decide that.

## The routine that makes it easy

What makes the report hard is almost always that it is put off until everything has to be remembered at once. Instead, write down every job you apply for on the same day. You can enter it directly in Mina sidor, or use [Applications](/applications) in Jobin to keep track of what you applied for and when. How to build a habit around this is in [Structure in your job search](/knowledge-base/article/struktur-jobbsokning).

## What do I do now

Check on Mina sidor when your next reporting window opens and closes. Start to note every activity on the same day that you do it. If something stops you from sending in the report in time: contact Arbetsförmedlingen before the deadline ends.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is an activity report?

Your report to Arbetsförmedlingen about what you have done to get a job during the month. It is sent on Mina sidor.

### Is it enough to write that I have applied for jobs?

No, the case officer should be able to see what you have done. One line for each job you applied for, with company, job and date, is enough.

### Do I have to report even if I take part in a program or do work training?

Yes, according to Arbetsförmedlingen you should report also if you do a work placement or work training. The conditions for compensation in a program are on a separate page at Arbetsförmedlingen, so ask your case officer if you are unsure what applies for your program.

### What do I do if I forgot to take notes during the month?

Go through your e-mail inbox, your sent applications and your calendar. Be honest if you do not remember everything. Never write an activity that did not happen.

### How do I send the report if I do not have e-legitimation?

Through a servicekontor, a form or by phone. See the section above.
