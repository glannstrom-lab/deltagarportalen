TITLE: SIUS: a support person when you start a new job
SUMMARY: What SIUS is, what a SIUS consultant can help you with before and after you start work, and how to bring it up with your case officer.

SIUS is a special support person for introduction and follow-up support. The support person has special knowledge of introduction methods and helps you get in contact with employers, settle in at a new workplace and manage the job. The support is reduced step by step and ends when you can do the work tasks yourself. It is [Arbetsförmedlingen](https://arbetsformedlingen.se) (the Swedish Public Employment Service) that decides if it suits you.

## What is SIUS

SIUS is a support within Arbetsförmedlingen's efforts for people with a disability. It is for people who need special support before a job. You must be registered as a job seeker at Arbetsförmedlingen.

You can find the support person under several names: SIUS consultant, support person or just "SIUS". Arbetsförmedlingen calls the person "a so-called SIUS". Who employs the support person, Arbetsförmedlingen or someone else, is not stated on the agency's page for job seekers, so ask your case officer (handläggare).

## SIUS is not the same as lönebidrag

It is easy to mix them up when you search for "SIUS lönebidrag":

- **SIUS** is a person who supports you on the way into work.
- **[Lönebidrag](/knowledge-base/article/lonebidrag-sa-fungerar-det)** (wage subsidy) is financial support to the employer, so that the job can be adapted.

They are different decisions and different supports. Arbetsförmedlingen decides which one or ones suit you, based on your situation, so bring up both with your case officer.

## What the support person can do

Arbetsförmedlingen describes that the support person can, among other things, help you contact employers and be introduced at the workplace. Otherwise the support is adapted to you. Questions worth asking:

- Can you come with me and help me explain which adjustments I need?
- Can you go through the work tasks with me in a way that works for me?
- Can you be there the first days?
- Who do I contact, and when, if something starts to be difficult?

You, the employer and the support person agree on how the introduction is planned and how big it will be. So it is an agreement, not a ready-made package.

## Two parts: before and after you start

**Introduktionsstödet** (introduction support) is given to introduce you into a job. During the introduction time before employment, you get the same benefit that you had as a job seeker, according to Arbetsförmedlingen.

**Uppföljningsstödet** (follow-up support) continues after the employment has started. The support stays for some time so that you and the employer have someone to turn to if something needs to be sorted out. How long the two parts can last is stated in the decision and on Arbetsförmedlingen's page. Ask your case officer what applies to you.

## How you become eligible

There is no form that you fill in yourself. Arbetsförmedlingen describes the steps like this:

1. You contact Arbetsförmedlingen.
2. You prepare to tell why the support can increase your chances of getting a job.
3. An arbetsförmedlare (a case officer at Arbetsförmedlingen) makes an assessment and looks at the work tasks and the support that may be needed.
4. You get in contact with an appointed SIUS after a decision.

Good times to bring it up are when you talk about why earlier jobs or internships did not work, when an employer has shown interest but is unsure what the job requires, or when it is not the work tasks themselves that are difficult but everything around them.

## Keep in mind

The support is adapted to the workplace and to you, not the other way around. If a place does not turn out right, that is information that you and the support person take with you to the next try. It is not a failure.

If you need help with the work environment itself, there are [Arbetshjälpmedel på jobbet](/knowledge-base/article/arbetshjalpmedel-pa-jobbet) (work aids at the job) and [Anpassningar på arbetsplatsen](/knowledge-base/article/anpassningar-arbetsplats) (adjustments at the workplace). Other supports that can exist alongside, for example interpreter and literature, are described in [Tolk och litteratur – stöd](/knowledge-base/article/tolk-och-litteratur-stod) (interpreter and literature: support).

## Common questions

### What is SIUS?

A special support person for introduction and follow-up support. The support helps you get in contact with employers, settle in at the workplace and manage the work tasks.

### Who is a SIUS consultant or SIUS case officer?

It is the support person you get through the decision. It is the arbetsförmedlare at Arbetsförmedlingen who decides if you should get the support, and who then makes sure you get in contact with an appointed support person.

### Does SIUS cost me anything?

Arbetsförmedlingen's page about the support does not state any costs for you. Ask your case officer if you want this confirmed.

### Does the employer have to know why I have a support person?

You and the support person agree on that. You can describe which adjustments you need without telling why.

### Are SIUS and metodik the same thing?

SIUS-metodik (SIUS method) is the introduction method that the support person has special knowledge of. Arbetsförmedlingen describes the support as introduction at the workplace.

## What do I do now

Bring up SIUS with your case officer, preferably before a job starts. Describe concretely what is usually difficult in the change to a new job. That is the basis for which support you are offered. More information is at [arbetsformedlingen.se](https://arbetsformedlingen.se).
