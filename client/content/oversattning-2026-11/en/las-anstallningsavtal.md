TITLE: Employment contract: read it and get help to check it
SUMMARY: What to check in an employment contract, which clauses are important, and where you can get help to review the contract.

An employment contract (anställningsavtal) is the agreement about your terms. You can ask the union if you are a member, an employment law lawyer, or the employer, who can explain the points. The employer must also give you written information about the most important terms. Read the contract calmly before you sign.

Here we go through what to look for. This text is a reading aid, not legal advice.

## Writing an employment contract

If you want to write an employment contract yourself, for example as an employer or because you hire help at home, it must be clear who the parties are, which form of employment it is, pay, working hours and when the job starts. A contract can be written freely, but LAS (lagen om anställningsskydd, the Employment Protection Act) sets requirements for which written information the employer must give. More about this is in [Anställningsbevis (written proof of employment)](/knowledge-base/article/anstallningsbevis). If you use a template, check it against the law and the kollektivavtal (collective agreement), and ask a lawyer or an employers' organisation.

## What to check

### Parties, workplace and form of employment
The employer's name and organisation number, your details, workplace and job title, start date and which form of employment it is. Is it tillsvidare (permanent) ("anställningsavtal tillsvidare"), visstid (for a fixed time) or prov (trial)? See [Forms of employment](/knowledge-base/article/anstallningsformer-guide) and [Trial employment](/knowledge-base/article/provanstallning-guide).

### Pay and benefits
How the pay is calculated, when it is paid, how and when it is reviewed, and if it is variable. Pension, insurance and other benefits. See [Pay and benefits](/knowledge-base/article/lon-och-formaner-guide) and [When the pay does not arrive](/knowledge-base/article/lonen-uteblir-vad-gor-jag).

### Working hours and holiday
How many hours per week, if there is on-call duty, overtime or flexible time, and if overtime is included in the pay. If it says förtroendearbetstid (working time based on trust), it can mean that the rules about working hours and overtime look different, so ask what it means at that employer. Holiday is governed by the semesterlagen (Annual Leave Act) and possibly a kollektivavtal.

### Notice period
What applies for you and for the employer, and if the contract follows the kollektivavtal. See [Notice period and notice of termination](/knowledge-base/article/uppsagningstid-och-uppsagningsbesked). How long the notice period is varies between law, kollektivavtal and contract, so look in your own.

### Kollektivavtal
Does it say which kollektivavtal applies, or that there is none? See [Kollektivavtal: what it means](/knowledge-base/article/kollektivavtal-vad-det-betyder).

## Clauses that are extra important to read

### Non-compete clause (konkurrensklausul)
Limits what you may do after the job, for example work for a competitor. Ask what counts as a competitor, how long it applies, if you get payment and if it is reasonable for your role. Ask the union if you are not sure.

### Confidentiality and NDA
NDA means non-disclosure agreement, that is, a confidentiality agreement. It means that you may not tell about the company's business secrets or internal information. Read how widely it is written, if it also applies after the job, and if there is a vite (a fine, an amount you must pay if you break it). Ask if something is unclear, especially if the text stops you from talking about working conditions with the union or a lawyer.

### Rights to what you create
Often it says that what you create in the job belongs to the employer. Check if this also applies to things you do in your free time, and ask for an exception for projects you already have.

## How to get help to review the contract

- **The union**: if you are a member you can ask them. See [The union: should I join?](/knowledge-base/article/facket-ska-jag-ga-med).
- **Employment law lawyer**: if you are not a member, you can check if your insurance has legal protection (rättsskydd), and ask a lawyer.
- **The employer** can explain every point. It is a reasonable question.

## Before you sign

1. Read the whole contract, also the attachments.
2. Ask questions. It shows that you take the job seriously.
3. Ask to take the contract home with you. You do not have to sign right away.
4. Ask the union or a lawyer if something worries you.
5. Negotiate. Much can be discussed.

Warning signs are pressure to sign right away, unclear wording and very broad clauses.

## What do I do now

Take out the contract, read it with the sections here as a list and mark what you do not understand. Send the questions to the employer or call the union.

## Common questions

### How can I get help to review an employment contract?
The union if you are a member, an employment law lawyer, or the employer, who can explain the points.

### Can someone help me check my employment contract?
Yes, see the section above about how to get help to review.

### Do I have to have a written contract?
The law requires that the employer gives you written information about the terms that are of essential importance for the job. This is what is called anställningsbevis. See [Anställningsbevis](/knowledge-base/article/anstallningsbevis).

### What does NDA mean?
Non-disclosure agreement, a confidentiality agreement. See the section about confidentiality above.

### Is there a free employment contract?
Templates can be found, but always check that the template matches the law and the kollektivavtal, and ask a lawyer or an employers' organisation if you are not sure.

### What is a normal notice period?
See the section about notice period above.
