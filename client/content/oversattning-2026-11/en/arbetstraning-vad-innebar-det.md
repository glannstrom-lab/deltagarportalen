TITLE: Arbetsträning (work training) – how it works and pay
SUMMARY: What arbetsträning is, who plans it, what applies to compensation and how it differs from förstärkt arbetsträning. Does it lead to a job?

Arbetsträning (work training) means that for a period you are at a workplace and practice work tasks, without it being a job and without a demand to perform like an employee. The purpose is to build work ability, energy and stamina. Here is how arbetsträning works, who plans it and what compensation there can be.

## What arbetsträning is

According to [Försäkringskassan (the Swedish Social Insurance Agency)](https://www.forsakringskassan.se), arbetsträning means that you come to a workplace without a demand to perform and get help with the work tasks that you cannot do because of your illness. Others do the tasks that you do not have time for or cannot do. The work training should not replace the time it normally takes to learn a new job.

It is often combined with other measures, for example adapted working hours or work aids. You can read more about them in [Work aids at work](/knowledge-base/article/arbetshjalpmedel-pa-jobbet).

## How arbetsträning works

It goes like this:

1. The employer or [Arbetsförmedlingen (the Swedish Public Employment Service)](https://arbetsformedlingen.se) plans the work training together with Försäkringskassan.
2. Försäkringskassan assesses if it is an effective rehabilitation measure for you in particular.
3. If it is, Försäkringskassan makes a plan together with the doctor, you and the employer or Arbetsförmedlingen.
4. You train at the workplace according to the plan.

The decision is based on your work ability and on what would be needed for it to increase, not on a diagnosis.

## Is arbetsträning a job

No. It is something different from a job, and that decides everything else:

| | Arbetsträning | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Pay from the workplace | No | Yes |
| Employment contract | No | Yes |
| Demand to perform | Adapted, no usual demand | Yes, according to the role |
| Purpose | Test and build ability | Do a job |

If you instead need a real job with support, there are for example [offentligt skyddat arbete (publicly protected employment)](/knowledge-base/article/offentligt-skyddat-arbete) and [lönebidrag (wage subsidy)](/knowledge-base/article/lonebidrag-sa-fungerar-det). If you are on your way back after sick leave, [Back to work after sick leave](/knowledge-base/article/tillbaka-till-jobb-efter-sjukskrivning) can be good to read at the same time.

## Arbetsträning and compensation

You do not get pay, but Försäkringskassan states that it can pay rehabiliteringsersättning (rehabilitation compensation) during the work training. If you have sjukpenning (sickness benefit), you can get both rehabiliteringsersättning and sjukpenning. If you have sjukersättning or aktivitetsersättning (long-term illness and activity compensation), you keep that instead. The conditions, for example how large a part of the day you should do work training to get rehabiliteringspenning, change, so ask your case officer at Försäkringskassan what applies to you.

If you take part through a program at Arbetsförmedlingen, you can apply for compensation at Försäkringskassan as aktivitetsstöd (activity support) or utvecklingsersättning (development compensation). See [Aktivitetsstöd – who pays during a program?](/knowledge-base/article/ersattning-under-program).

If you are registered as a job seeker, you should according to Arbetsförmedlingen send in an activity report even when you do work training. See [Activity report – how to do it](/knowledge-base/article/aktivitetsrapport-guide).

## Arbetsträning and förstärkt arbetsträning

The two are often mixed up. Förstärkt arbetsträning (reinforced work training) is a measure at Arbetsförmedlingen with more guidance, and it can last longer. It is for you who take part in jobb- och utvecklingsgarantin (the job and development guarantee), jobbgarantin för ungdomar (the job guarantee for young people) or etableringsprogrammet (the establishment programme). Read [Förstärkt arbetsträning](/knowledge-base/article/forstarkt-arbetstraning). Arbetsträning in the general sense, as above, can also take place within Försäkringskassan's rehabilitation.

A simpler language is in [Arbetsträning – easy Swedish](/knowledge-base/article/lattsvenska-arbetstraning).

## What you can ask for from the guidance

Work training without working guidance is just being at a workplace. You can ask for:

- a clear contact person at the workplace
- to know what is expected before you start a task
- check-ins along the way, not only at the beginning and the end
- that the load is adapted if you say it is too high

If none of this works: bring it up with the person who planned the work training, that is the case officer at Försäkringskassan or Arbetsförmedlingen.

## Does arbetsträning lead to a job

It depends on why the place was chosen. There is no guarantee. A place where the employer has a real need and sees you as a possible candidate has a different direction than a place that just happens to be free. Therefore ask early if there is a possibility of employment behind it. Also write down how the shifts feel week by week, so you can see if the load really goes in the right direction. The next step after arbetsträning can be [work placement](/knowledge-base/article/praktik-som-vag-in) or a job with support.

## Frequently asked questions

### How does arbetsträning work?

You come to a workplace and practice tasks with adapted demands, according to a plan that Försäkringskassan or Arbetsförmedlingen has made together with you and the employer.

### Do I get pay during arbetsträning?

No, but Försäkringskassan can pay rehabiliteringsersättning, or you keep your sjukersättning or aktivitetsersättning. Försäkringskassan decides what applies to you.

### Can I say no to a place?

It depends on who assigned it and what compensation you have. Talk to the case officer before you say no, because a no can have consequences for the compensation. Tell them why the place does not suit you, so that the next suggestion can be better.

### How long does the work training last?

It is decided by your plan and your needs. Your case officer can say what applies to you.

### What is arbetsträning at Arbetsförmedlingen?

See the section about förstärkt arbetsträning above.

## What do I do now

Ask your case officer to explain why exactly that place is suggested, and ask straight out if there is a job behind it. Also ask what compensation you have in the meantime. Read more about work-focused rehabilitation at [forsakringskassan.se](https://www.forsakringskassan.se) and about arbetsträning through Arbetsförmedlingen at [arbetsformedlingen.se](https://arbetsformedlingen.se).
