TITLE: What does praktik mean? A simple explanation
SUMMARY: Praktik means you are at a workplace to learn a job. A simple explanation of pay, compensation and who decides.

Praktik (work placement) means that you are at a workplace for a time to learn how a job works. You are not employed. A person who does praktik is called a praktikant. Another word for praktik is arbetspraktik.

## Do I get pay during praktik?

If you do praktik through [Arbetsförmedlingen](https://arbetsformedlingen.se) (the Swedish Public Employment Service), you are not employed. Then the workplace does not pay any pay.

You can get compensation instead. It can be called aktivitetsstöd, utvecklingsersättning or etableringsersättning. [Försäkringskassan](https://www.forsakringskassan.se) (the Swedish Social Insurance Agency) pays it out.

If you arrange the praktik yourself, it is a different matter. Ask what applies before you start.

## Who decides if I get praktik?

Praktik through Arbetsförmedlingen is planned by you together with your handläggare (case officer) and the employer.

Talk to your handläggare about what suits you.

## What can I learn on a praktik?

- How a job works in real life
- New knowledge
- How a workplace in Sweden works
- To speak Swedish in everyday life, if you practice

## Can praktik lead to a job?

Sometimes. It is not certain.

You can do a lot yourself:

- Come on time every day.
- Ask if you are unsure about a task.
- Show that you want to learn.
- Tell them that you are interested in jobs there.

## Is praktik the same as arbetsträning?

No. On a praktik you try a job. In an [arbetsträning](/knowledge-base/article/lattsvenska-arbetstraning) (work training) you practice working, with more support.

## What do I do now?

Talk to your handläggare about whether praktik can suit you. During the praktik: come on time, ask when you wonder about something and show that you want to learn.

Do you want to read more? Read [Praktik as a way into working life](/knowledge-base/article/praktik-som-vag-in).

## Common questions

### Must I accept a praktik place that I am offered?

Talk to your handläggare before you say no. A no to a praktik that Arbetsförmedlingen has referred you to can have consequences for your compensation. Also ask your a-kassa. A-kassa means unemployment insurance fund.

### Am I insured during praktik?

Yes, if you do praktik through Arbetsförmedlingen. Then you are covered by personskadeförsäkring (personal injury insurance) and grupplivförsäkring (group life insurance). Ask your handläggare what this means for you.

### What happens if I get sick during praktik?

Report sick to Arbetsförmedlingen on the first day. Otherwise you can lose the compensation for that time.
