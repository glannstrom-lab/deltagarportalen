TITLE: Free CV template – simple template and examples
SUMMARY: A simple CV template to copy, what goes in each part, and examples for little experience, a gap or a new field. Plus a free CV builder.

A simple CV template is a CV with the same five parts in the same order: contact details, profile, work experience, education and skills. Here you get the structure, text you can copy and fill in, and examples for different situations. If you want to avoid the layout, there is a free CV builder in Jobin with 12 templates and PDF export.

## Free CV template: choose how you want to do it

There are three ways, and all of them work:

- **Copy the template further down** and paste it into Word or Google Docs. Then you control everything yourself.
- **Use the CV builder in Jobin.** You fill in your details once, choose a template among 12 and download a finished PDF. The tools in Jobin are free and you need an account with an e-mail address.
- **Start from an earlier CV** and change what is no longer correct.

Which way you choose matters less than that the content is true and easy to read.

## Simple CV template to copy

Fill in the square brackets and remove what does not fit:

> **[First name Last name]**
> [Phone] | [E-mail] | [City]
>
> **Profile**
> [Two to four sentences: who you are, what you can do, what you are looking for.]
>
> **Work experience**
> [Title], [Workplace] | [Month year] – [Month year]
> - [What you did and what you were responsible for]
> - [Something you achieved, if you can say what]
>
> **Education**
> [Education], [School] | [Year]
>
> **Skills**
> [Programs, tools, driving licence, languages and level]

The latest job and the latest education come first. Contact details should be an address and a number that you really answer.

## What goes in each part

### Profile

A few sentences at the top that answer: who are you in working life, what can you do and what are you looking for. Write it last and adapt it to the job you are applying for.

### Work experience

Write what you did and what the result was. "Responsible for the cash register" says something, but "Responsible for the cash register and the check at closing" says more. If you have numbers for what you achieved and you can stand behind them, include them. Never make up numbers that sound good.

### Education and skills

Include what is relevant for the job. Languages, driving licence and computer programs belong here. Write the level the way you would describe it to a colleague.

## Templates for different situations

### You have worked before

Highlight what is most like the job you are applying for. In the profile: your job, your field, two or three things you are good at. In the experience: what shows that you can do what the ad asks for.

### You have little or no experience

Experience is more than jobs. Work placements, unpaid work for a good cause, club tasks, school projects, your own projects and responsibility at home can all be a basis. Write what you did, not only where. There is a separate guide: [Writing a CV without formal experience](/knowledge-base/article/cv-utan-erfarenhet).

### You are changing field

Show what you bring with you: tasks and skills that work in the new area. Say in the profile where you are going. You do not need to explain why in detail, you can do that in the cover letter.

### You have a gap in your CV

It is possible to write something true and short. See [Gaps in your CV](/knowledge-base/article/luckor-i-cv).

### You have experience from another country

See [CV with work experience from abroad](/knowledge-base/article/utlandsk-erfarenhet-i-cv).

## Examples of how to describe what you have done

Replace the square brackets with your own details. Write only what is true.

- "Received and handled [what] in [system or channel]."
- "Was responsible for [area of responsibility] at [workplace]."
- "Introduced [routine or improvement], which [what became better]."
- "Trained [number] new colleagues in [task]."
- "Planned and carried out [activity] for [whom]."

## Common mistakes

- General statements without support, such as "I am good at customer contact". Describe a situation instead.
- One and the same CV for all jobs. Adjust the profile and highlight what is relevant.
- Spelling mistakes. Read aloud and let someone else read it.
- Details you cannot stand behind. A CV that is not true will come out in the interview.

How long the CV should be and whether it should have a photo have their own guides: [How long should a CV be?](/knowledge-base/article/hur-langt-ska-ett-cv-vara) and [Photo on your CV or not?](/knowledge-base/article/bild-pa-cv-eller-inte). Which personal details belong there is in [Which personal details should be in your CV?](/knowledge-base/article/personuppgifter-i-cv).

## If the CV will be read by a recruitment system

If you apply through a form, the CV may be read by a program first. A simple template with one column and ordinary headings, like the one above, is easiest. Read more in [ATS systems – how they work and how to adapt your CV](/knowledge-base/article/ats-system-guide).

## What do I do now

Copy the template and fill in contact details and work experience first. Let the profile wait. Or open the CV builder, fill in once and try different templates. Ask someone you trust to read it before you send it. If you need a cover letter, there is [Cover letter – templates and examples](/knowledge-base/article/personligt-brev-mall).

## Frequently asked questions

### Is there a free CV template?

Yes. You can copy the template above to any word processing program. In Jobin there is also a free CV builder with 12 templates, where you download the CV as a PDF.

### What is a simple CV template?

A CV with one column, ordinary headings and no pictures or graphics, where the latest item comes first. The template above is one of those.

### Can I have several different CVs?

Yes. You can have a basic CV that you adapt to each application. In the CV builder you can save several versions.

### Do I need to know computers to make a CV?

You need to be able to fill in a form. Layout, fonts and page breaks are handled by the CV builder.
