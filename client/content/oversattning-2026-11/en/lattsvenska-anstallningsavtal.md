TITLE: What is an anställningsavtal? A simple explanation
SUMMARY: An anställningsavtal shows what you and the employer have agreed. A simple explanation of what should be in it before you sign.

An anställningsavtal (employment contract) shows what you and the employer have agreed. It says, for example, the job, the pay and the working hours. It is also called jobbavtal or arbetsavtal.

## Must the contract be in writing?

No. A contract can also be spoken. Then it is harder to show what you agreed.

The employer must still give you written information about your terms. Ask for it.

## What is an anställningsbevis?

An anställningsbevis (employment certificate) is a paper where the employer writes down the terms of your job. It is the same kind of information that the employer must give you in writing.

## What should be in the contract?

- Which job and which tasks you have
- Where you work
- When you start
- If the job is permanent, has an end date or is on trial
- What pay you get
- How much you will work

## What does "on trial" mean?

A job on trial is called provanställning (probationary employment). You and the employer try each other for a time. Read more about [provanställning](/knowledge-base/article/lattsvenska-provanstallning).

It should say clearly in the contract if the job is on trial.

## Must I sign right away?

You can ask for time to read the contract. Take it home and read it one more time.

If you are in the union (facket), they can read the contract before you sign. Read more about [the union](/knowledge-base/article/lattsvenska-facket).

## What do I do if something is unclear or wrong?

Ask the employer directly. Do not sign if you do not understand what it says.

## Can the contract be digital?

Yes. A contract can be a paper or a file on the computer. Ask for a copy in both cases.

## Do I get my own copy?

Always ask for a copy when you have signed. Keep it. You may need it later, for example if something goes wrong with the pay.

## What do I do now?

Read the whole contract before you sign. Check the job, working hours, pay, start date and if the job is permanent or on trial. Ask for a copy.

Do you want to read more? Read [How to read an employment contract: clause by clause](/knowledge-base/article/las-anstallningsavtal).

## Common questions

### Can I get help to read the contract?

Yes. If you are in the union, they can help you. You can also ask a person you trust to read it with you.

### How long does the employer have to give me the information?

The law sets a time limit. The most important information should come early, the rest a little later. If you do not have it, ask the employer or the union.
