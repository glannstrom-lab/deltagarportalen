TITLE: Etableringsprogrammet: what is included and who is covered
SUMMARY: What etableringsprogrammet is, which measures are included, who decides what, and what applies if you do not show up. Read before your next meeting.

Etableringsprogrammet (the establishment program) is Arbetsförmedlingen's program for newly arrived people who recently got a residence permit as a refugee or person in need of protection, or who are family members of someone who has one. The goal is that you, as quickly as possible, learn Swedish, find a job and support yourself. Here is who the program is for, what is included and who decides what.

## What is etableringsprogrammet

It is a labour market program at [Arbetsförmedlingen (the Swedish Public Employment Service)](https://arbetsformedlingen.se). You get measures adapted to you as an individual and at the same time you actively look for a job. You plan the measures together with a case officer, and an interpreter can take part when needed.

The program is usually full-time. Part-time can be possible, for example during parental leave, part-time work, or illness and disability.

## Who is covered

Arbetsförmedlingen describes the target group as newly arrived people who have received a residence permit as a refugee or person in need of protection, and their family members. It is Arbetsförmedlingen that decides if you belong to the group and meet the other conditions. If you are not sure, ask your case officer or read at arbetsformedlingen.se. Age limits and other details change, so we do not write them here.

## What is included

The building blocks vary between people. Arbetsförmedlingen mentions, for example:

- **Sfi**, svenska för invandrare (Swedish for immigrants), for those who do not have basic knowledge of Swedish. The teaching is organised by the municipality.
- **Samhällsorienterande utbildning** (civic orientation), which explains how Swedish society works.
- **Education at different levels**, for example komvux (municipal adult education).
- **Work placement** and **job search support**.
- **Support for starting your own business** and **validation** of what you can do.

The measures should run in parallel, not one after the other. So you do not have to be finished with sfi to start with something else. How this fits together is described in [Looking for a job while you study SFI](/knowledge-base/article/sfi-och-jobbsokande-samtidigt).

### Education duty

If you are covered by the education duty (utbildningsplikt), you must apply for studies at komvux in your municipality or for a general course at a folkhögskola (folk high school). Ask your case officer if you are covered.

## Who decides what

| Who | Responsible for |
|---|---|
| Arbetsförmedlingen | The program, your action plan and the work preparation measures |
| The municipality | Sfi and education at komvux |
| Försäkringskassan (the Swedish Social Insurance Agency) | Pays out the etableringsersättning (establishment benefit) |
| You | Taking part, coming to meetings and telling what you want to achieve |

You make your action plan (handlingsplan) together with your case officer. Earlier it was called etableringsplan, and you may see both words. Read more about how the meeting works in [Your planning at Arbetsförmedlingen](/knowledge-base/article/din-planering-hos-arbetsformedlingen).

The money during the program is etableringsersättning, and it is paid by Försäkringskassan. See [Etableringsersättning](/knowledge-base/article/etableringsersattning).

## Stricter rules if you do not show up

Since 1 June 2026 you can be left without benefit right away, already the first time, if you are absent from the program without a valid reason, or if you do not register for sfi or apply for an education that was listed as a mandatory activity in your action plan. Arbetsförmedlingen says that they always check why you did not take part, and that they contact you before they make a decision. Read more in [Warning from Arbetsförmedlingen](/knowledge-base/article/varning-fran-arbetsformedlingen).

Get in touch in advance if you cannot take part. If you are ill, you must tell Arbetsförmedlingen on the first day of illness.

## How long, and what happens after

The length of the program time is limited, and there is a time frame counted from when you got your personnummer (Swedish personal identity number). The exact times are on Arbetsförmedlingen's page. You can stay until you start working full-time, go on full-time parental leave, or become ill full-time and the program cannot be adjusted. Arbetsförmedlingen can also withdraw the program, for example in case of misconduct or if you no longer belong to the target group.

Talk about the next step in good time. It can be a job, studies or another measure at Arbetsförmedlingen. There is also support for newly arrived people who have already started working, see [Etableringsjobb](/knowledge-base/article/etableringsjobb).

## How to get more from the time

- Ask for your education and experience to be mapped early. If you have a foreign education, read [Validating a foreign education](/knowledge-base/article/validera-utlandsk-utbildning).
- Ask about a work placement in the field you are aiming for.
- Ask questions in the civic orientation. It is your time.
- Keep in touch with your case officer between meetings if something changes.

## What do I do now

Book a meeting with your case officer if you do not know what is included in your own plan. Ask if the mapping is done and if the placement you are offered is close to what you want to work with.

## Common questions

### What is etableringsprogrammet?

A program at Arbetsförmedlingen for newly arrived people, with sfi, civic orientation, work placement and other measures in an individual plan.

### Do I have to take part in the whole program?

You must take part in what is in your action plan. If your situation changes, for example if you get a job, the plan changes. Talk to your case officer about what applies to you, and what happens with the benefit.

### Can I say no to a measure that does not suit me?

Bring it up with your case officer before you opt out. If you do not show up for something in the plan without a valid reason, it can lead to you being left without benefit, and it is Arbetsförmedlingen that assesses what is valid.

### I already know some Swedish. What do I do?

Tell your case officer and your teachers at sfi. The teaching should start from the level you have.
