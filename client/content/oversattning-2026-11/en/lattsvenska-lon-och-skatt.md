TITLE: What do brutto and netto mean? Simple explanation
SUMMARY: Brutto is pay before tax, netto is pay after tax. A simple explanation of pay, A-skatt and lönebesked, and what to do if your pay is wrong.

Brutto means before tax. Bruttolön (gross pay) is your pay before the tax is taken out. Netto means after tax. Nettolön (net pay) is what comes to your bank account.

## What is the difference between brutto and netto?

Bruttolön is pay before tax. It is also called pay before tax. You can see it in your contract or in a job ad.

Nettolön is what is left after the tax. Nettolön is lower than bruttolön.

Always ask if a pay that someone mentions is brutto or netto.

## What is tax and A-skatt?

Tax is money that goes to the state and the municipality. It is used for things like schools, health care and roads.

A-skatt (tax taken from your pay) is the tax that your employer takes out of your pay. The employer pays it in for you to [Skatteverket (the Swedish Tax Agency)](https://www.skatteverket.se). You do not have to pay it yourself.

## How much tax do I pay?

It depends on how high your pay is and in which municipality you live. Skatteverket has tax tables. The employer uses them.

Read more in [Tax on pay – how to read your first lönebesked](/knowledge-base/article/skatt-pa-lon-forsta-jobbet).

## What is a lönebesked?

A lönebesked (pay slip) is what you get every time you get paid. It is also called lönespecifikation.

There you can see:

- how much you have worked
- your bruttolön
- how much tax has been taken out
- your nettolön

## When does the pay come?

The date is in your employment contract. Ask the employer if you are unsure.

## What do I do if the pay is wrong?

First check your lönebesked. Are the hours right? Is the amount right?

If something is wrong, contact your manager or the person who handles the pay. Do it as soon as you see the mistake.

If you are a member of the union, they can also help you. Did the pay not come at all? Read [The pay did not come – what to do now](/knowledge-base/article/lonen-uteblir-vad-gor-jag).

## What do I do now?

Save all your lönebesked. Check every time that hours and amounts are right. Get in touch right away if something is wrong.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I have to pay the tax myself?

No. Your employer takes out the tax and pays it in for you.

### Where do I get answers to questions about tax?

Skatteverket answers questions about tax. You ask questions about your pay to the employer.
