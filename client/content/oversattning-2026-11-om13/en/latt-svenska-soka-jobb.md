TITLE: How do you apply for jobs? A simple step-by-step guide
SUMMARY: How to apply for a job in six steps, from finding an ad to sending the application. Written in easy language.

To apply for a job means that you send an application to a company that wants to hire. Here is a simple guide in steps. You can take one step at a time.

Do you want to read more? There is a longer guide: [Help with applying for jobs: who can help you?](/knowledge-base/article/hjalp-att-soka-jobb)

## Step 1: Find jobs

You can find jobs here:
- On this site, Jobin. Use the [job search](/job-search).
- At [Arbetsförmedlingen](https://arbetsformedlingen.se) (the Swedish Public Employment Service)
- On the companies' own websites
- On LinkedIn

## Step 2: Read the ad

Read the ad carefully. Look at:
- What will you do at work?
- What do you need to know?
- Where is the job?
- When does the job start?
- How and when should you send your application?

Read more: [How do I read a job ad?](/knowledge-base/article/lattsvenska-lasa-jobbannons)

## Step 3: Get your CV ready

Your CV should fit the job.
Add what the company asks for in the ad.
Read more: [What should be in a CV?](/knowledge-base/article/latt-svenska-cv)

## Step 4: Write a letter

Write a short letter. It is called a personligt brev (cover letter).
Tell:
- who you are
- why you want the job
- why you are right for the job

You can get help in the tool [Cover letter](/cover-letter).

## Step 5: Send the application

Send your CV and your letter.
Often you do this on a website or by email.
Do as it says in the ad.

## Step 6: Wait

Now you have to wait. The answer can take time.
Apply for more jobs while you wait.
You can write down your applications in [Applications](/applications).

## If you get an answer

Maybe you can come to an interview.
An interview is a meeting where the manager talks to you.
Read more: [What happens at an interview?](/knowledge-base/article/latt-svenska-intervju)

## If you do not get an answer

That is okay. Sometimes no answer comes.
Keep applying for other jobs.
Every application is good practice.

## Tips

- Apply for many jobs.
- Ask your handledare (support person) or arbetskonsulent (employment consultant) for help.
- Take breaks when you are tired.
