TITLE: What is a job interview? How it works
SUMMARY: What a job interview is, what questions you can get and how to prepare, step by step, in easy language.

A job interview is a meeting. You meet a manager or someone else from the company. They want to get to know you and ask questions about the job. You can also ask questions.

Do you want to read more? There is a longer guide: [Prepare for the interview](/knowledge-base/article/intervju-forberedelser)

## What is an interview?

An interview is also called an arbetsintervju. It can be at the company, on the phone or on video.

The company wants to know who you are and what you can do. You want to know if the job is right for you.

## Before the interview

**The day before:**
- Find out where the interview is.
- Read the job ad again.
- Choose clothes that feel good for you.
- Sleep as well as you can.

**The same day:**
- Eat something.
- Be on time. It is good to come a little early.
- Bring your CV.

Do you want to practise? Read [Interview training: how to practise](/knowledge-base/article/intervjutraning-sa-ovar-du).

## What does the manager ask?

The manager asks questions. These are common questions.

**"Tell me about yourself"**
Say:
- what your name is
- what you have done before
- why you are applying for the job

Read more: [Tell me about yourself](/knowledge-base/article/beratta-om-dig-sjalv).

**"Why do you want to work here?"**
Say something good about the company.
For example: "I like that you..."

**"What are you good at?"**
Tell about something you can do.
For example: "I am good at..."

**"Do you have questions?"**
Ask the manager a question.
For example: "What does a normal day look like?"

## Tips during the interview

- Look at the person when you talk.
- Listen carefully to the questions.
- Answer calmly. You do not need to hurry.
- It is okay to say "Can you explain the question?"

## After the interview

You can send a short message.
Write: "Thank you for the interview. It was nice to meet you."

## If you are nervous

It is a common feeling to be nervous before an interview.
Breathe slowly.
Think: "I will do my best."

Do you have a handledare (support person) or an arbetskonsulent (employment consultant)? Ask if you can practise together.
You can also practise in the [interview simulator](/interview-simulator).
