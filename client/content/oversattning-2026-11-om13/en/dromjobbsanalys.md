TITLE: Dream job analysis: step by step
SUMMARY: Describe your dream job, compare it with what you have and make a first plan. Exercises and a gap analysis you can do at your own pace.

A dream job is easier to reach when you know what it is made of. This guide helps you describe what you are looking for, compare it with what you already have and make a first plan. You do not need to know the answer today. The dream can change along the way.

## Why do a dream job analysis?

It is easier to apply for the right jobs when you know what you are looking for. A clear picture helps you choose which ads to spend time on, which courses are worth it and what you want to ask about at an interview. If you are not sure at all what you want to do, you can start with [Find your dream job: a reflection guide](/knowledge-base/article/hitta-ditt-drommjobb) or [What should I work with?](/knowledge-base/article/vad-ska-jag-jobba-med).

## Part 1: Describe the dream

### The perfect working day

Imagine a normal working day in a few years and write it down in your own words.

- **Morning:** How do you get to work, or do you work from home? When do you start?
- **Day:** Where are you: inside, outside, travelling? What tasks do you do? Are you alone, in a team or with customers?
- **Evening:** When do you finish? How do you feel? What do you have energy for afterwards?

### Questions to answer quickly

**About the work:** Which tasks do you want to do every day? Which do you want to avoid? Do you want to work with people, data, things or ideas? Do you want routine or variety?

**About the place:** Inside or outside? A small or a big company? Public or private? Near or far from home?

**About life around work:** How important is flexibility? How much work do you have the strength and wish for? What does your economy need to manage?

**About meaning:** What do you want to give? Which values must the employer share? What would make you proud?

There are no wrong answers. The answers show what is important to you.

## Part 2: Gap analysis: what is needed, and what do you have?

Look at 5 to 10 job ads for the role or similar roles. Write down what comes back again and again: education, certificates, technical skills, experience and personal qualities. Then fill in a table about yourself:

| Area | What the role needs | What I have | What is missing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Education | | | |
| Experience | | | |
| Skills | | | |
| Qualities | | | |
| Contacts | | | |
| Permits and licences | | | |

Sort what is missing into three piles:

1. **Must have**: without it you cannot get the job.
2. **Good to have**: it increases your chances.
3. **Can wait**: an advantage, but not decisive.

Jobin has a tool for exactly this: the [skills gap analysis](/skills-gap-analysis).

## Part 3: Is the dream possible right now?

Some questions that help you be honest with yourself, without locking yourself in:

- Are there jobs in the field, and where are they? Look in the [job search](/job-search) and in [Jobs of the future](/knowledge-base/article/framtidens-jobb).
- Does the way there fit your life situation? Think about energy, money, family and health.
- Are there entry roles or nearby roles that lead there?

Talk to someone who does the job, if you can. This is called an informationsintervju (information interview), and you can read how in [Information interview: your secret job search strategy](/knowledge-base/article/informationsintervju-guide). Ask questions like what everyday life looks like, what is good and bad and how they got there.

You may adjust. Maybe you find a similar role that fits better, or you see that the dream was not what you thought. That is a result, not a failure.

## Part 4: Make a plan

Break the plan into steps you can manage. Here is an example of the form. Change it to fit your situation and your energy:

**This week**
- Write down your dream job description.
- Read some ads for the role.
- Write down who you can talk to.

**This month**
- Talk to a person who has the job.
- Look at a course or a way to get experience.
- Update your CV and profile. The [CV builder](/cv) helps you.

**Later**
- Get what is missing, for example education, praktik (work placement) or experience. Read about [Education](/education) and [Validation of professional skills](/knowledge-base/article/validering-av-yrkeskunskaper).
- Apply for jobs or praktik that bring you closer.

Celebrate small steps: the first talk, the first course, the first application.

## What do I do now

Write the perfect working day on half a page. It takes a short time and is the only step you need to take today. You can take the rest at your own pace.

## Common questions

### How do I find my dream job?

Start by describing what you want to do, where and with whom. Then look at which jobs contain that. See [Find your dream job](/knowledge-base/article/hitta-ditt-drommjobb) and Jobin's [interest guide](/interest-guide).

### What do I do if I do not know what I dream about?

Not knowing is not wrong. Start with what you want to avoid and what gives you energy. That is a good start.

### Must the dream job be perfect?

No. See it as a direction. A job can be on the way there without being the goal.
