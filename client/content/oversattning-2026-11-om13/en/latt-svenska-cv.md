TITLE: What is a CV and what should be in it?
SUMMARY: A CV is a paper about you when you apply for jobs. Here you see what to write in it, step by step, in easy language.

A CV is a paper about you. It shows who you are, what jobs you have had and what you can do. You send it when you apply for a job. CV means curriculum vitae. It is Latin and means about "the course of life".

Do you want to read more? There is a longer guide: [How to write a CV that gets results](/knowledge-base/article/cv-grunder)

## Why do you need a CV?

When you apply for a job, the manager wants to know:
- What is your name?
- What work have you done before?
- What have you learned at school?
- What are you good at?

Your CV answers those questions.

## What should be in a CV?

**1. Your name**
Write your first name and last name.

**2. Phone number**
So the manager can call you.

**3. Email**
So the manager can write to you.

**4. Your jobs**
Write which jobs you have had.
Write the latest job first.

**5. Your education**
Write which school you went to.
Write what you studied.

**6. What you can do**
Write what you are good at.
For example: driving licence, computers, languages.

## Tips

- Keep your CV short.
- Write clearly.
- Ask someone to read your CV.
- Change your CV so it fits the job you apply for.
