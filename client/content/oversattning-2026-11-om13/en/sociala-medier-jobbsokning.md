TITLE: Recruitment through social media: how to use it
SUMMARY: How employers can use social media and how you find jobs there, protect your privacy and clean up what you do not want to be seen.

Recruitment through social media means two things: employers can find you, and you can find them. Here is how you use Instagram, X, TikTok and Facebook in your job search and protect your privacy, without making your private life into a CV.

## How employers can use social media

Employers can google a candidate, look at public profiles or advertise open jobs on social media. How it is done varies, and you cannot know in advance what one employer does. What you can influence is what is public. Read more in [Google yourself: what do employers see?](/knowledge-base/article/googla-dig-sjalv).

## Three ways to think

1. **Check** what is public.
2. **Choose** what you want to show and what should be private.
3. **Use** the channels to find jobs and contacts, not only to be seen.

## Find jobs through social media

- **Follow companies** you want to work for. They can show open jobs and what the workplace looks like.
- **Join industry groups** on Facebook or other platforms. Open jobs and tips can be shared there.
- **Search local groups** with words like "jobb" (job) and the name of your town.
- **Always check the ad** on the employer's own website or in a job service before you give personal information. Never write your personnummer (Swedish personal identity number) or bank details in a chat or comment.
- The explanation of [how recruitment works](/knowledge-base/article/rekryteringsprocessen-steg-for-steg) helps you understand when a contact is serious.

## Instagram

- Follow companies and people in your industry and comment in a factual way.
- Show work, projects or interests that are connected to what you are looking for.
- Choose a username and a profile photo that you are comfortable with an employer seeing.
- If you want to limit who sees your photos, you can set the account to private in the settings.
- Check photos where you are tagged and remove what you do not want connected to you.

## X

- Write a clear biography with your profession or what you are looking for.
- Follow the industry and take part in discussions that you can stand behind.
- Remember that old posts can be found. Clean up what you do not want to follow you.

## TikTok

- Companies can show the workplace and everyday life in short clips, which gives a picture of the place.
- It can fit you if you are looking in a field where you want to show something and are comfortable in front of the camera.
- If you do not want to be seen: set the account to private and think about what you share.

## Facebook

- Groups for an industry or a town can be a source of tips and contacts.
- Follow company pages that you are interested in.
- Check your settings for who sees your posts, older posts and how you can be found.

## Do a clean-up

1. Google your name and see what comes up.
2. List your accounts, also old and forgotten ones.
3. Divide them: public and professional, private, and those you want to remove.
4. Remove or hide what you do not want to be seen.
5. Look at the profile photo and biography on the accounts you leave public.

More about this in [Clean up and improve your digital presence](/knowledge-base/article/digital-narvaro-rensning).

## If an employer asks for your accounts

It is your choice what you want to share. You can answer in a friendly way that you keep your private life private and point to the ones you want to show, for example LinkedIn. If you get a question that feels wrong, you can read about your rights in [Discrimination when applying for jobs](/knowledge-base/article/diskriminering-jobbsokning). Diskrimineringsombudsmannen (the Equality Ombudsman) is at [do.se](https://www.do.se).

## What do I do now

Google your name, go through your accounts and decide which can be public. Update LinkedIn if you have one, see the [LinkedIn optimiser](/linkedin-optimizer).

## Common questions

### Do employers check social media?

It can happen. How often and how varies between employers, and you cannot know in advance. That is why it is wise to know what is public.

### How do I find jobs through social media?

Follow companies, join industry groups and search by town and industry. Check the ad with the employer before you give information.

### Do I have to have LinkedIn?

No, but it fits many jobs. Read [Complete guide to LinkedIn optimisation](/knowledge-base/article/linkedin-optimering).

### Can I hide my accounts?

Yes. You can set them to private, change the name or remove them. See the clean-up above.
