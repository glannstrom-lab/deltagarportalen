Getting Started
How do I create a CV?

Go to the CV section and follow the step-by-step guide. You can choose from different templates and get AI help with the wording.

Go to getting started
What is the Interest Guide?

The Interest Guide helps you find careers that match your personality and interests. It takes about 10 minutes to complete.

Go to getting started
How do I save jobs?

When searching for jobs, you can click the heart/save button on each job ad to save it to your job tracker.

Go to getting started
CV & Cover Letter
Can I have multiple CV versions?

Yes! You can save multiple versions of your CV and choose which one to use for each application.

Go to cv & cover letter
How does the AI letter writer work?

The AI analyzes the job ad and your CV to write a cover letter that matches that specific job.

Go to cv & cover letter
What is ATS analysis?

ATS (Applicant Tracking System) is software that many companies use to filter CVs. Our analysis helps you optimize your CV to get past these systems.

Go to cv & cover letter
Job Search
How do I search for jobs through the portal?

Use the job search function to search among job openings. You can filter by location, profession, and employment type.

Go to job search
What's the difference between saving and applying?

When you save a job, it's added to your job tracker so you can return to it later. Applications are usually made through the company's website.

Go to job search
Can I get notifications about new jobs?

Yes, you can set up job alerts in your settings to receive emails when new jobs matching your criteria are published.

Go to job search

How do the Exercises work?

The exercises are interactive tools that help you practice various aspects of job searching, like selling yourself or preparing for interviews.
